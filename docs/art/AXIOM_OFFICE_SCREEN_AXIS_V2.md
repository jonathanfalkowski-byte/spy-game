# Axiom office screen axis — style V2

Use this screen direction for the playable arrival and desk conversations. It is a shot-continuity guide, not a change to M5's structural environment authority or an approval of an unreviewed image.

- From the arrival camera, Adrian enters on screen left and moves or looks screen right toward his desk.
- The desk's long front edge faces the camera. Its tabletop, front panel, legs, and executive chair behind it must remain readable as dimensional furniture.
- The two visitor chairs and shared workstations stay screen left. The blank terminal stays at the desk's screen-right end. The smoked-glass director's office and door stay screen right behind the desk.
- In desk conversations, keep the camera on this side of the room's action line. Adrian occupies the left side of his seated frame and looks right. Visitors approach from the circulation side; do not mirror the desk, swap the monitor and chairs, or move the glass office to the opposite wall for a reverse shot.
- A camera change may tighten the framing, but the character's gaze, desk geometry, entrances, and prop custody must still join to the preceding shot. Use a true reverse only if the room remains spatially legible and the cut is motivated by action.

The arrival order is rainy exterior approach, lobby badge/security gate, elevator threshold and walk toward the desk, then Daniel at the desk. The security frame may show Adrian's coat and phone in a screening tray. The office-arrival frame occurs after he retrieves them and before Daniel is visually active. No Benton slate, Maya coffee, or Blackglass file appears early.

Style: mature prestige graphic novel with nuanced semi-cel faces, restrained ink, physical depth, cool graphite surfaces, warm practical light, and consequential shadows. Adrian remains an ordinary bookish man with thin rectangular glasses; the staging should not glamorize him into a model.
