## Architecture, materials and scale

V: pale gray square tile grid, cool cream/plaster walls, dark base trim, matte charcoal
cabinet fronts, light stone-like counter and black metal window frame. P: same floor
and finishes continue through the living/kitchen circulation; bedroom uses muted gray
carpet and the bathroom pale tile. No marble palace, double-height atrium, gold trim,
glass stair or futuristic control room. Upscale employer-supported efficiency, modest
footprint, coordinated generic furniture, few personal layers.

P scale guides, to be checked in the master rather than treated as exact measurements:
ordinary 2.5–2.7 m ceiling; about 0.9 m kitchen counter; 0.43–0.46 m chair seat;
roughly 2.0 m sofa; at least 0.8–0.9 m clear circulation. Use door, counter, chair and
tile-grid perspective together. Camera is around standing chest/eye height, 1.45–1.60 m,
with a natural 35–45 mm-equivalent field of view. No fisheye stretching. Character
feet must share the floor plane; a hand contacting a drawer, coat or mirror must be
within plausible reach. Existing image scale is a starting check, not metric proof.
