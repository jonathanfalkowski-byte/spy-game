## Adrian visual north star — supersedes the broad direction

Adrian Vale is thirty-four and highly intelligent, valued for what he notices rather than accustomed to being noticed. He should read as capable, pleasant-looking, slightly bookish, professionally credible, socially guarded, modest in presentation, and easy to overlook in a corporate crowd. He is not ugly, weak, unhealthy, pathetic, childish, a caricatured nerd, an action hero, a fashion model, a superhero, or an alpha-male fantasy.

His body direction is lean or average with slightly narrow shoulders, ordinary adult proportions, a modest physical footprint, and contained professional posture. His shoulders are natural rather than squared, his hands practical rather than performative, and his expression thoughtful and observant rather than brooding or intense. The contrast with Evelynn comes from life experience and social visibility, never from making Adrian inferior.

Adrian and Evelynn may share subtle eye, brow, nose, cheek, and jaw lineage, but they must not read as simple gender-swapped twins. Evelynn's later beauty, recognition, confidence, influence, attention, and public visibility are meaningful rewards that coexist with surveillance, objectification, dependency, exploitation, loss of privacy, and external control. The game leaves the question of why Evelynn might want to give that life up to the player.
