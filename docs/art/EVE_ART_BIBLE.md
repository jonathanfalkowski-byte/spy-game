# EVE — ART BIBLE v1.0

**Status:** Canonical Visual Direction  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Authority:** All production artwork should follow this document unless a later approved art-bible version supersedes it.

## VISUAL THESIS

EVE is a **dark adult espionage graphic novel** presented through polished 2D animation-style illustration.

The visual language combines:

**prestige graphic novel + sophisticated adult animation + classic spy-poster elegance + modern cinematic noir**

The art should feel:

**Elegant. Dangerous. Adult. Seductive. Controlled. Expensive. Psychological.**

EVE must NOT look like photorealistic AI art, generic anime, a dating simulator, a comedy cartoon, or a superhero comic.

The world is glamorous on the surface and dangerous underneath.

---

# CORE STYLE

## Rendering

- polished 2D illustration
- clean, confident linework
- semi-cel shading
- strong graphic shadows
- controlled highlights
- crisp silhouettes
- cinematic depth
- selective environmental detail
- subtle texture rather than photographic rendering

Characters should appear illustrated, not rendered as photographs.

Skin should use graphic shading and controlled highlights rather than pores or hyperrealistic texture.

## Character proportions

Adult characters use elegant, believable anatomy with slight fashion-illustration exaggeration.

Favor:

- long clean lines
- strong silhouettes
- elegant posture
- defined shoulders
- expressive hands
- distinctive profiles
- controlled physical confidence

Avoid caricature or extreme body exaggeration unless intentionally character-specific.

---

# CHARACTER FACES

Major characters must be recognizable from silhouette and face alone.

Favor:

- angular facial structure
- distinctive cheekbones
- expressive eyes
- strong brows
- controlled mouth shapes
- recognizable profiles
- restrained facial acting

Expressions should communicate emotion without becoming cartoonishly exaggerated.

Important expressions include:

- controlled neutrality
- suspicion
- amusement
- calculation
- attraction
- anger
- fear
- vulnerability
- contempt
- confidence
- emotional fracture

Characters frequently conceal what they actually feel.

Their face should therefore allow **subtle performance**.

---

# EVELYNN

Evelynn is the primary visual anchor of EVE.

She should immediately communicate:

**intelligence + elegance + danger + control**

She is striking without looking artificial.

Her appearance should be capable of communicating radically different psychological states without changing her fundamental character design.

## Canonical visual direction

Adult woman.

Dark brunette / near-black hair.

Sharp, elegant facial structure.

Defined cheekbones.

Almond-shaped expressive eyes.

Strong brows.

Elegant nose.

Full but controlled lips.

Slim, graceful adult build.

Long clean silhouette.

Confident posture.

Her beauty should read as **dangerous and sophisticated rather than innocent or cute**.

## Canonical gala presentation

The current Evelynn Helix gala illustration establishes the initial reference direction.

Key characteristics:

- dark brunette elegant updo
- loose face-framing strands
- black backless evening gown
- black gemstone earrings
- subtle sophisticated makeup
- champagne flute
- dark clutch
- poised over-the-shoulder stance
- black and gold environment
- city lights
- Helix corporate event
- composed, observant expression

Future representations of Evelynn should remain visually recognizable relative to this design.

# COLOR

Primary world palette:

- black
- charcoal
- gunmetal
- cream
- midnight blue
- burgundy
- deep emerald
- champagne gold
- dark red
- warm amber

Color is narrative.

### Power / luxury
Black + gold.

### Danger
Black + controlled red.

### Surveillance / isolation
Cold blue + gray.

### Clinical environments
White + steel + pale cyan.

### Intimacy / vulnerability
Warmer, softer practical lighting without abandoning noir contrast.

Avoid excessive rainbow palettes or generic cyberpunk neon.

---

# LIGHTING

Lighting is part of storytelling.

Preferred lighting:

- noir shadow
- warm practical lamps
- city glow
- window light
- rim lighting
- candlelight
- hard directional office lighting
- reflected glass light
- nightclub practical lighting
- controlled pools of darkness

Lighting should help communicate:

- power
- danger
- vulnerability
- surveillance
- deception
- intimacy
- isolation

# ENVIRONMENTS

The world of EVE should feel affluent, controlled and slightly intimidating.

Core locations may include:

- corporate galas
- luxury hotels
- private clubs
- penthouses
- embassies
- private clinics
- modern corporate offices
- yachts
- high-end restaurants
- intelligence facilities
- nightclubs
- safehouses
- executive vehicles
- airports/private aviation
- expensive residences

Luxury should feel believable rather than gaudy.

There should usually be something in the composition suggesting observation, power, secrecy or restricted access.

---

# WARDROBE

Clothing communicates character, status and intention.

Evelynn's wardrobe should include:

### Formal
Elegant gowns, sophisticated eveningwear and high-end accessories.

### Operational
Sharp, practical clothing that still preserves her silhouette and identity.

### Business
Tailored jackets, fitted trousers, skirts and dresses with understated luxury.

### Private
More relaxed clothing while maintaining character consistency.

Wardrobe may change presentation dramatically without changing Evelynn's underlying physical design.

Materials include:

- silk
- satin
- velvet
- fine wool
- leather
- polished metal
- restrained jewelry

Avoid costume-like "spy outfits."

---

# CAMERA LANGUAGE

Treat important illustrations like film shots rather than character pinups.

Preferred framing:

- cinematic wides
- 3/4 character shots
- over-the-shoulder compositions
- full-body establishing shots
- close facial reactions
- medium dialogue framing
- controlled low-angle power shots
- restrained high-angle vulnerability shots

Camera position should support story psychology.

Characters do not always face the viewer.

Use:

- foreground obstruction
- reflections
- doorways
- glass
- mirrors
- silhouettes
- negative space

when appropriate to reinforce surveillance and divided identity.

---

# ADULT TONE

EVE is an adult story.

Sensuality should feel integrated into character, power, relationships and espionage rather than pasted onto the story.

Visual presentation can range from restrained to mature depending on the scene, but the underlying art direction remains:

**cinematic, sophisticated, psychologically grounded and character-driven.**

The same characters must remain recognizable regardless of presentation intensity.

---

# STYLE CONSISTENCY RULE

Every generated or commissioned EVE image should pass this test:

> Could this image plausibly be a frame from the same sophisticated animated espionage series as the canonical Evelynn Helix gala image?

If not, reject or revise it.

---

# DO NOT DRIFT INTO

- photorealistic AI photography
- generic anime
- Pixar/Disney aesthetics
- superhero comic styling
- goofy adult-animation parody
- cheap pin-up illustration
- exaggerated cartoon anatomy
- plastic CGI skin
- random art styles between scenes
- excessive neon cyberpunk
- overly busy backgrounds
- generic fantasy glamour

---

# PRODUCTION PRIORITY

Consistency is more important than individual-image perfection.

For important characters establish:

1. canonical face
2. front view
3. 3/4 view
4. profile
5. full-body proportions
6. expression sheet
7. canonical wardrobe
8. alternate wardrobe
9. lighting references
10. approved character assets

Only after the design is stable should dedicated character-generation training or LoRA-style adaptation be considered.

---

## EVE VISUAL RULE

**Beauty attracts attention.**

**Composition creates tension.**

**Lighting reveals power.**

**Expression tells the truth the dialogue does not.**
