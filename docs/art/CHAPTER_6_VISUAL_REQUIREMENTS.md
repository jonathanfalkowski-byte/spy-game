# Chapter 6 visual requirements — The Cage You Choose

**Design only.** No image is generated, promoted, bound to the reader, or treated as character canon by this document. Use the locked reader shell and the global rule: **HOLD ON DIALOGUE. CUT ON ACTION.**

## Production constraints

- Reuse a master only when location, time, wardrobe, participants, props and completed action remain truthful.
- A dialogue hold cannot cross an arrival, departure, handed-over artifact, wardrobe change, consent boundary, public release or resolved physical action.
- Existing Chapter 5 apartment and Harbour approvals are exact-state assets. They are not universal Chapter 6 masters.
- New environment work follows `ENVIRONMENT MASTER → CHARACTER LAYERS → PROP LAYERS → DETERMINISTIC COMPOSITOR`.
- A visual may show only knowledge and objects that the relevant branch has earned. No public artifact, private message, agreement or person appears early.
- Design for a small reusable family per location; do not create a new angle for every speaker or emotion.
