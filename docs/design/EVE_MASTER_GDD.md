# EVE — Master Game Design Document

**Full-Game North Star — Version 0.3**

This document governs future full-game design. The historical vertical-slice GDD remains valid production evidence for the opening through Glass House, but it intentionally deferred Chapters 3+, long-term relationships, celebrity, adult entertainment, deeper body modification, dependency, and endgame route structure.

EVE is a **dark adult espionage role-playing game** about investigation, identity, desire, autonomy, power, and the life Evelynn builds after being forced into an identity that was prepared for her.

The player investigates incomplete evidence and decides:
- what Evelynn believes;
- what she wants;
- what she performs;
- what she accepts or refuses;
- who she trusts;
- who she uses;
- who uses her;
- what she is willing to trade for access, money, safety, status, intimacy, pleasure, fame, and power.

No single trajectory is automatically the moral answer.

## 1. Core player promise

EVE must function simultaneously as:

1. **Espionage game** — investigation, evidence, source reliability, custody, leverage, infiltration, counterintelligence.
2. **Identity game** — Adrian/Evelynn, self-authorship, presentation, public identity, body, later transformation choices.
3. **Power game** — money, housing, employment, contracts, resources, obligation, dependency, institutions, leverage.
4. **Desire game** — attraction, affection, sex, luxury, attention, fame, ambition, appetite, intimacy.

These systems must collide. Evelynn may discover damaging evidence about someone she desires, loves, depends on, works for, is married to, or needs for access.

## 2. Core gameplay loop

**INVESTIGATE → ASSESS → ACT → CONSEQUENCE**

### Investigate
Observe people, records, documents, spaces, social patterns, claims, opportunities, and contradictions. Important scenes may contain mutually exclusive investigative opportunities; the player is not always allowed to exhaust every option.

### Assess
Assessment records what the player believes, not what the game knows. Canonical truth, player knowledge, player assessment, and player report remain separate.

### Act
The player may disclose, conceal, lie, confront, negotiate, protect, exploit, preserve, destroy, trade, seduce, refuse, withdraw, or wait.

### Consequence
Choices change knowledge, relationships, evidence ownership, exposure, access, resources, obligations, public identity, body state, NPC belief, and future options.

A wrong theory may still create a valid story. A correct theory may still lead to a disastrous decision.

## 3. Information model

Evidence is not truth.

Player-facing evidence should preserve, where relevant:
- source;
- type: physical, documentary, digital, testimony, observation, public record;
- provenance;
- custody;
- corroboration;
- conflict;
- exposure: who knows Evelynn has or knows it.

Avoid visible numeric reliability percentages. The player should decide what to trust.

Evidence custody matters. Seeing a record, photographing it, stealing an original, routing audio to Sloane, keeping a token, or giving someone the only copy are different game states.

## 4. Darkness and agency

**EVE does not guarantee that Evelynn remains in control. It guarantees that the game remembers how control changed hands.**

The game may portray:
- manipulation;
- coercion;
- threats;
- blackmail;
- financial control;
- isolation;
- exploitative contracts;
- professional abuse;
- controlling or abusive relationships;
- bodily pressure;
- forced compliance;
- cruelty;
- loss of meaningful alternatives;
- exploitation and recovery.

Track separately:
- consent;
- compliance;
- desire;
- pleasure;
- affection;
- dependency;
- control;
- strategy.

These are never interchangeable.

A later positive feeling never retroactively converts coercion into consent. A person may comply without consenting, enjoy part of a situation they did not freely choose, love someone who controls them, or strategically remain in a restrictive arrangement.

## 5. Thematic laws

1. Becoming female is not corruption.
2. Erotic desire is not a morality score.
3. Adult work is not inherently exploitation.
4. Luxury is not automatically dependency.
5. Dependency is defined by shrinking meaningful alternatives.
6. Consent, compliance, desire, pleasure, affection, dependency, control, and strategy remain separate facts.
7. A harmful event remains harmful even if Evelynn later values some consequence of it.
8. Evelynn may become victim, survivor, manipulator, protector, exploiter, executive, partner, dependent, or power broker.
9. The player is not guaranteed control forever.
10. The game remembers how control was gained, offered, surrendered, negotiated, manipulated, taken, resisted, exploited, or recovered.
11. Body appearance never reveals whether a change was desired.
12. Relationships may be loving, strategic, transactional, dependent, abusive, or mixed.
13. Julian is not automatically savior or villain.
14. Celebrity is both opportunity and exposure.
15. Adult entertainment can be autonomous career, corporate business, public power, or exploitation depending on actual conditions.
16. Trophy-wife or kept-life appearances do not reveal who actually holds power.
17. Investigation remains playable on every major trajectory.
18. Major branches may reconverge without erasing prior consequences.
19. Refusal, negotiation, or recovery should remain possible where the fiction still permits those options.
20. Dark outcomes are allowed to remain dark.

## 6. Body autonomy and modification

Body change is a separate system axis, not a route label.

Possible sources:
- self-directed;
- career-directed;
- sponsored;
- relationship-directed;
- dependency-pressured;
- coerced or imposed.

Track, when relevant:
- requested_by;
- desired_by_evelynn;
- pressure_source;
- who pays;
- reversibility;
- permanence;
- accepted scope;
- resulting appearance;
- who believes they gained influence over Evelynn's body.

Possible categories:
- styling;
- hair;
- cosmetic changes;
- body shaping;
- cosmetic procedures;
- surgical changes;
- tattoos or piercings where appropriate;
- later EVE-stage medical changes.

The same final body state may mean self-authorship, strategy, pressure, dependency, or abuse depending on how it happened.

Later irreversible EVE stages retain their established affirmative-authorization rule unless separately changed through explicit canon approval.

## 7. Route families

Routes emerge from accumulated state; they are not selected from a menu.

### A. Espionage / Agency
- Free Agent
- Sloane Operative
- Rook-aligned

Shared content: investigations, missions, ORACLE, source networks, counterintelligence, evidence custody.

Differences: handler, objectives, support, who owns proof, loyalty, autonomy, and what each side knows.

### B. Corporate / Private Power
- Helix power
- Corporate Predator
- Executive Companion
- Trophy Wife
- Kept / Dependent

The same outwardly luxurious life may represent genuine partnership, strategic marriage, social power, economic dependency, image control, abuse, or an apparently ornamental role hiding substantial leverage.

### C. Public / Body / Fame
- Celebrity Evelynn
- Public figure
- Model / editorial career
- Adult Entertainment / porn-star career

Shared systems: representation, image rights, contracts, publicity, sponsorship, body/image expectations, compensation, ownership, audience, scandal, privacy.

Adult entertainment is not inherently the dark path. A high-autonomy performer may own rights, build wealth, become mainstream-famous, form a company, use public access for intelligence, and leave on her own terms. The same industry can also support exploitation, coercive management, contract traps, blackmail, humiliating publicity, bodily pressure, and dependency.

### D. Exploitation / Recovery overlay
This can emerge from any family and is not necessarily a faction route.

Triggers require actual conditions: concentrated resources, threats, blackmail, isolation, contract control, bodily control, abusive relationship, reputation dependence, or compromised evidence.

Outcomes may include deeper exploitation, accommodation, strategic survival, counter-manipulation, retaliation, escape, help, recovery, or Evelynn becoming controlling herself.

Recovery means restoring meaningful alternatives and authorship. It does not require rejection of femininity, fame, adult work, luxury, marriage, or transformation.

## 8. Route crossover

Profession does not equal faction.

A celebrity Evelynn may also be:
- Julian's partner;
- a Sloane operative;
- a Free Agent;
- an adult performer;
- a corporate owner.

An adult-entertainment Evelynn may become:
- mainstream celebrity;
- business owner;
- intelligence asset;
- independent operative;
- controlled performer;
- executive partner.

A trophy-wife Evelynn may secretly be:
- a Free Agent;
- corporate strategist;
- Rook ally;
- intelligence broker;
- dependent victim;
- dominant partner.

## 9. Campaign length target

Target one substantial first-playthrough major path at **15–20 hours**.

Do not build 15–20 unique hours for every trajectory.

Provisional budget:
- **Shared foundation:** 6–7 hours
- **Route-shaped development:** 6–8 hours
- **Convergent endgame:** 3–5 hours

These remain production targets pending playtests.

## 10. Branch and reconvergence law

Avoid exponential story trees.

Use diamond branching:

**Diverge → Persist → Reconverge → React**

A shared mission may still differ in:
- access;
- wardrobe/body;
- companion;
- resources;
- public recognition;
- evidence;
- attraction;
- obligations;
- vulnerability;
- dialogue;
- adult-scene eligibility.

Reconvergence never means pretending prior branches did not happen.

## 11. Adult thriller pacing

After Chapter 3, substantial chapters should normally contain **2–4 adult heat beats**. These may involve:
- attraction;
- flirting;
- seduction;
- self-presentation;
- being desired;
- provocative presentation;
- public-image exposure;
- erotic professional opportunity;
- private invitation;
- adult nightlife;
- relationship power;
- luxury/dependency;
- body/image negotiation;
- jealousy;
- intimacy;
- manipulation.

Usually **0–2** become actual intimate handoffs depending on state.

Do not insert sex to meet a quota. Every beat must serve character, investigation, identity, power, relationship, or route progression.

Julian must not own all adult content. Non-Julian, independent, public, and Free Agent players need adult-life possibilities too.

## 12. Adult content and coercion boundary

Mutually authorized intimate outcomes may use AdultSceneSpec.

Coercive or abusive events:
- remain explicitly non-consensual in state;
- are not classified as mutually willing AdultSceneSpec outcomes;
- remain non-graphic in canonical runtime;
- may produce substantial aftermath, evidence, leverage, relationship, and recovery consequences.

The game may be severe without confusing abuse with consent.

## 13. Investigation survives every route

No route becomes only lifestyle content.

Every major chapter retains:

**INVESTIGATE → ASSESS → ACT → CONSEQUENCE**

Different trajectories change access:
- celebrity: press, events, staff, visibility;
- trophy wife: private rooms, executive networks, household access;
- adult performer: industry contacts, producers, events, public/private access;
- Sloane operative: institutional tools and surveillance;
- Free Agent: independent sources, public records, paid access, personal evidence.

Same conspiracy. Different access.

## 14. Full-game trajectory ladders

### Celebrity
Public profile → representation → sponsorship → high visibility → image pressure → either ownership or management control.

### Adult entertainment
Professional offer → bounded first work → rights/distribution choices → career/reputation → either independent business power or increasing external control.

### Executive / Trophy
Relationship → shared resources → public role → residence/travel → commitment/marriage → partnership, strategic power, or dependency.

### Corporate
Professional influence → executive authority → intelligence access → ability to control other people's opportunities.

### Exploitation
One dependency → multiple dependencies → real exit cost → loss of alternatives → survival/counterpower/recovery or deeper control.

## 15. Production discipline

- Preserve historical revisions.
- Do not infer route locks from one choice.
- Do not infer consent from appearance or profession.
- Do not let public images create universal NPC knowledge.
- Preserve source-aware NPC knowledge.
- Keep route state inspectable.
- Reuse environments and composition families where possible.
- Art follows state: hold on dialogue, cut on meaningful action/evidentiary change.
- Adult and dark content must remain consequence-bearing, not decorative.

## 16. Current chapter direction

### Chapter 4 — Private Access
Preserve the existing Julian professional/personal progression, but future full-game design should add at least one non-Julian adult-life opportunity so Evelynn's sexuality and social confidence can exist outside Sloane and Julian. Charm or attraction may become optional social leverage with adult targets, but core evidence must remain available through non-intimate routes.

### Chapter 5 — The Beautiful Life
Preserve Dress for Yourself, Harbour attention, Aster, and Wanting Something. Expand future design so public attention and adult life are not Julian-only. Aster may seed broader editorial, luxury, sensual-professional, or representation opportunities without route-locking Evelynn into adult work.

### Chapter 6 — The Cage You Choose
Chapter 6 should be investigation-heavy and route-sensitive. It needs explicit design lanes for:
- executive/Julian relationship power;
- celebrity/public image;
- adult-entertainment career seed;
- seduction-as-tradecraft;
- dependency/exploitation.

The chapter should test which benefits have become costly to leave while the Rook/ORACLE proof chain gives Evelynn counterpower.
