# Future trajectories

Design direction only. These are possible long-term patterns, not implemented routes, new canon, chapter outlines or single-choice endings. They do not change the vertical slice, content version 9 or save schema 5. A future trajectory would emerge through repeated choices, obligations, opportunities and relationships; no outfit, profession, gender expression or isolated interaction selects one automatically.

## Design law

Sexual expression, adult entertainment, fame, femininity and luxury are **not themselves corruption**. Appetite describes desired attention, sensation, comfort, status or experience; it does not measure moral worth. Harm to others is a separate question from appearance or profession.

The darker movement occurs when autonomy decreases, dependency increases, external control replaces meaningful choice, and leverage or coercion closes options. Dependency must be considered concretely: money, housing, safety, legal status, access, information or attachment. Chosen interdependence with viable alternatives is not identical to imposed dependence.

A voluntary adult-entertainment career and exploitation are distinct trajectories. Executive companionship and ornamental dependency are also distinct. A companion arrangement can become dependency through accumulated obligations and control, but that change is neither inevitable nor established by intimacy. Recovery restores options and authorship; it does not require repudiating femininity, pleasure, a profession or a chosen identity.

Reconnections below are possibilities for later design, never promised scenes or evidence that an organization already controls a trajectory. The player may refuse reconnection. Rook remains an unresolved actor in the playable slice; this design document does not reveal her identity to players.

## 1. Free Agent

- **Core fantasy:** choose one's own work, allies and limits while remaining capable in a dangerous intelligence world.
- **Autonomy level:** high, maintained through alternatives and the ability to walk away rather than mere defiance.
- **Appetite pattern:** self-directed; austerity, luxury or pleasure may all fit.
- **Ambition pattern:** competence, independence, reputation and ownership of decisions.
- **Dependency pattern:** deliberately diversified resources and alliances; independence may still require reciprocal help.
- **Power source:** information, skill, credibility, chosen networks and evidence under personal control.
- **Primary cost:** fewer institutional protections and more personal responsibility for risk and livelihood.
- **Relationship consequences:** negotiate trust and boundaries; allies may dislike unreliable access to the protagonist.
- **Espionage consequence:** selective contracts, contested intelligence and difficult decisions about what to sell, retain or expose.
- **Recovery/reintegration:** rebuild alliances or accept bounded institutional support without surrendering all discretion.
- **Reconnection:** Axiom or Helix could be clients or adversaries; Rook a negotiated ally; Sloane a counterpart or conditional handler. None automatically regains command.

## 2. Sloane Operative

- **Core fantasy:** excel at dangerous work within an unusually capable handler's operation while contesting its terms.
- **Autonomy level:** constrained and negotiated; outward cooperation can conceal independent aims.
- **Appetite pattern:** may favor competence, danger, recognition or controlled luxury; no required intimate preference.
- **Ambition pattern:** operational mastery, access and increasing influence over mission selection.
- **Dependency pattern:** support, credentials, protection and privileged information concentrate around the handler.
- **Power source:** institutional reach combined with personal usefulness and retained bargaining options.
- **Primary cost:** surveillance, withheld context, compromised exit options and complicity in others' objectives.
- **Relationship consequences:** secrecy can strain outside ties; professional loyalty need not become emotional submission.
- **Espionage consequence:** deeper access and support, accompanied by possible conflict between mission success and the protagonist's interests.
- **Recovery/reintegration:** renegotiate authority, retain independent evidence, establish outside support or move into free agency.
- **Reconnection:** Axiom/Sloane are the likely institutional connection; Helix and Rook could offer alternatives or intelligence. Their trustworthiness remains a story question, not a route guarantee.

## 3. Corporate Predator

- **Core fantasy:** become a consequential decision-maker who can shape institutions instead of merely serving them.
- **Autonomy level:** potentially high, but constrained by boards, financing, alliances and exposure.
- **Appetite pattern:** status, victory, ownership and access; private tastes remain open.
- **Ambition pattern:** direct executive power, strategic control and durable influence.
- **Dependency pattern:** capital and gatekeepers can replace a handler as sources of constraint.
- **Power source:** corporate authority, intelligence advantages, assets and coalition-building.
- **Primary cost:** obligations to stakeholders and the temptation to sacrifice other people's choices for results.
- **Relationship consequences:** trust may become transactional; the trajectory must allow protective leadership as well as exploitation. The title is a fantasy of competitiveness, not proof that every action is cruel.
- **Espionage consequence:** intelligence becomes a strategic business resource; privileged access also makes the protagonist a target.
- **Recovery/reintegration:** relinquish a harmful bargain, share power, restore damaged trust or leave an institution while retaining expertise.
- **Reconnection:** Axiom, Helix and Sloane could negotiate, compete or seek influence; Rook could supply a competing account. No executive promotion or acquisition is assumed now.

## 4. Celebrity Power

- **Core fantasy:** turn public recognition and a carefully authored image into influence.
- **Autonomy level:** variable; control of work and public identity matters more than visibility alone.
- **Appetite pattern:** attention, expression, celebration, novelty or luxury, without automatic degradation.
- **Ambition pattern:** cultural influence, reach, professional accomplishment and ownership of one's image.
- **Dependency pattern:** platforms, agents, sponsors, audiences and reputation may constrain choices.
- **Power source:** public legitimacy, access, attention and a network beyond intelligence institutions.
- **Primary cost:** privacy, scrutiny, loss of anonymity and pressure to keep performing a marketable self.
- **Relationship consequences:** distinguish genuine support from image management and access-seeking; fame need not erase intimacy.
- **Espionage consequence:** exceptional social access but reduced ability to disappear or act unnoticed.
- **Recovery/reintegration:** reduce exposure, regain image rights, diversify income or negotiate a sustainable public/private boundary.
- **Reconnection:** Axiom, Helix or Sloane could seek public access or influence; Rook could offer a covert link. Reconnection must account for recognizability rather than treating fame as invisibility.

## 5. Adult Entertainment Career

> the public-image themes. Kept below for history only.

- **Core fantasy:** pursue a chosen adult profession with ownership of work, boundaries, income and public presentation.
- **Autonomy level:** high when participation, terms, partners, distribution and departure remain meaningfully chosen.
- **Appetite pattern:** expression, attention, enterprise, experience or financial security; none implies a fixed identity or unlimited willingness.
- **Ambition pattern:** professional success, creative control, entrepreneurship or a sustainable livelihood.
- **Dependency pattern:** distributors, representation, financing and contracts can create risks, but risk is not automatic exploitation.
- **Power source:** owned work, audience, professional networks, bargaining strength and independent resources.
- **Primary cost:** exposure, privacy tradeoffs, stigma and the burden of maintaining enforceable boundaries.
- **Relationship consequences:** negotiated privacy and support; occupation must not dictate betrayal, attraction, availability or relationship failure.
- **Espionage consequence:** distinctive access and information networks alongside a visible public footprint. Work itself is not evidence of espionage or grounds for treating the character as compromised.
- **Recovery/reintegration:** change terms, retain rights, diversify, pause or leave the profession if desired. A continuing voluntary career is also a valid destination; no moral rehabilitation is required.
- **Reconnection:** Axiom/Helix/Sloane could intersect through information or professional access; Rook through a chosen alliance. Any attempt at blackmail is a separate act by a specific actor, not an intrinsic consequence of the career.

## 6. Executive Companion

- **Core fantasy:** exercise social influence and negotiate a valuable role close to powerful people.
- **Autonomy level:** negotiated; boundaries, independent resources and a viable exit distinguish partnership from captivity.
- **Appetite pattern:** company, access, luxury, status or chosen intimacy; no universal motivation is imposed.
- **Ambition pattern:** influence through access, counsel, introductions or an independently chosen relationship.
- **Dependency pattern:** benefits may come from one powerful person, but independent rights and resources can preserve choice.
- **Power source:** trust, discretion, social skill, strategic insight and proximity to decisions.
- **Primary cost:** unequal bargaining power, restricted privacy and the risk that one's own aims become secondary.
- **Relationship consequences:** boundaries and reciprocity need continuing negotiation; affection and professional arrangements must remain distinct facts.
- **Espionage consequence:** close access to informal decisions with surveillance and conflict-of-interest risks.
- **Recovery/reintegration:** renegotiate terms, establish independent income and allies, transition to another role or end the arrangement without a prescribed identity change.
- **Reconnection:** Axiom, Helix or Sloane could intersect through the executive's interests; Rook could be an outside contact. No specific executive or intimate relationship is assigned at this stage.

## 7. Ornamental Dependency / Kept Life

- **Core fantasy:** comfort, relief from responsibility and curated luxury, complicated by the accumulating price of reliance.
- **Autonomy level:** initially variable; the defining risk is the erosion of alternatives, not enjoying support.
- **Appetite pattern:** comfort, security, attention or ease can make restrictive bargains appealing; these desires are not themselves corrupt.
- **Ambition pattern:** personal ambitions may narrow, be deferred or become conditional on another person's approval.
- **Dependency pattern:** housing, money, social contacts, safety or mobility concentrate under external control.
- **Power source:** access and resources lent by a patron, with potentially weak independent control over them.
- **Primary cost:** reduced ability to refuse, leave, pursue goals or maintain relationships independently.
- **Relationship consequences:** isolation, conditional affection or unequal obligations may emerge through specific actions. Comfortable interdependence with respected boundaries should not be mislabeled as this darker pattern.
- **Espionage consequence:** access may persist while initiative and reporting channels become controlled; the protagonist may be treated as an asset rather than an actor.
- **Recovery/reintegration:** recover documents, resources, allies and realistic choices; gradual restoration is possible without requiring sudden rejection of every comfort.
- **Reconnection:** Axiom, Helix or Sloane might offer another dependency or a negotiated exit; Rook might help or pursue her own aims. Rescue must not automatically transfer ownership to a new handler.

## 8. Exploitation and Recovery

- **Core fantasy:** survive imposed control and recover authorship, safety and meaningful alternatives. Exploitation is the conflict, not the reward for a profession or gender expression.
- **Autonomy level:** reduced by specific coercion, deception, force or constrained choices; recovery progressively restores options.
- **Appetite pattern:** irrelevant to whether exploitation occurred. Prior enthusiasm, luxury, fame or past agreement does not authorize later abuse.
- **Ambition pattern:** may shift toward survival, justice, protecting others or rebuilding a chosen life; the player retains interpretive space.
- **Dependency pattern:** threats, isolation, financial control or withheld resources make refusal and departure costly.
- **Power source:** initially others' control; recovery may draw on evidence, alliances, material support, legal standing or regained skills.
- **Primary cost:** harm, constrained opportunity and damaged trust, portrayed without equating femininity or sexuality with injury.
- **Relationship consequences:** support may require time and boundaries; help does not purchase intimacy, loyalty or forgiveness.
- **Espionage consequence:** exploitation can damage cover and judgment or generate evidence against an exploiter; it must not automatically become a convenient upgrade or mission reward.
- **Recovery/reintegration:** authored paths to safety, resources, chosen relationships and work. Returning to a voluntary career, leaving it, or declining intelligence work can all remain legitimate.
- **Reconnection:** Axiom, Helix, Rook or Sloane might provide resources, pose threats or negotiate involvement. Any reconnection must preserve the option to refuse and must not erase the history of control.

## Early dark-power seeds — Chapter 3 design amendment

Design only; the frozen external-playtest slice remains unchanged. See [Second Skin](../story/CHAPTER_3_SECOND_SKIN.md), [Dark Power Roadmap](../story/DARK_POWER_ROADMAP.md) and [handoff map](../story/ADULT_SCENE_HANDOFF_MAP.md). Chapter 3 dark-power direction is approved, including residential surveillance and persistent authorized public association. Marcus's record frames a real discussion as more advanced interest; it must not fabricate definitive advisory acceptance. Future mechanisms and detailed treatment branches remain design proposals.

**Escalate situations quickly; escalate permanent loss of autonomy gradually.** Each major chapter should aim for an information problem, power confrontation, memorable dark situation, prior-behavior consequence and a valuable opportunity with a cost. Darkness can be surveillance, omission, status pressure, temptation or the protagonist's own exercise of leverage. No sexual scene quota or visible alignment meter follows.

|---|---|---|---|
| Free Agent | Question monitoring scope; negotiate independent access; retain bounded evidence | Diversified support and compatible commitments | Independence needs real alternatives, not one defiant answer |
| Sloane Operative | Accept specified reporting/support arrangements | Institutional access becomes useful and recurrent | Cooperation is not loyalty or unrestricted surveillance permission |
| Rook alignment | Verify a selected truth and decide what to report back | Reliance on one source can become informational dependency | Authentic information does not establish full candor; compare sources or withhold |
| Corporate Predator | Correct, preserve, trade or pressure using Marcus's favorable interpretation of a real exchange | Control of incomplete information and other people's access/reputations | Marcus may qualify it himself, defend his wording, refuse, restrict future access or reveal the player's actual demands; no automatic victory |
| Celebrity Power | Approve a particular public association | Recognition brings influence and costs anonymity | Publication is a durable artifact, not instant fame or blanket rights |
| Adult Entertainment Career | Preserve authorship over public image and future professional terms | Later voluntary business/public exposure choices, if independently earned | No career offer in Chapter 3; public visibility is neither sexual availability nor corruption |
| Executive Companion | Experience useful proximity and a respected boundary | Private access, favors and chosen comfort can develop gradually | No intimacy or enduring obligation inferred from reception attendance |
| Ornamental Dependency / Kept Life | Notice the terms attached to convenience or support | Specific obligations and concentrated resources can narrow alternatives | Luxury alone is not dependency; terms can be refused or unbundled |
| Exploitation and Recovery | Identify actual threats/limits and seek practical alternatives | Coercion can close choices; recovery reopens particular options | Separate from voluntary work and intimacy; no automatic escalation from either |

Rook alignment is an additional design direction, not a newly implemented route. Its appeal is actionable truth; its danger is dependence on a person who chooses which truths arrive. Sloane can tell the truth while constraining options, and Helix can offer genuinely useful help. No faction becomes the universal moral answer.

Publicity, favors and leverage need sourced histories. Distinguish potential, disclosed, threatened and exercised leverage. An agreed obligation is separate from another person's expectation of gratitude. Receiving help must have concrete consequences, but not an invented unlimited debt. The player can protect, conceal, trade, expose or pressure; consequences concern actual conduct rather than a good/evil label.

The proposed male executive may progress through respect, access, private meetings, favors, gifts, protection, expectations, obligations and influence over presentation/schedule. Dependency or isolation occurs only if alternatives actually narrow. High-autonomy or mutual relationships can stop or reverse that progression. Neither character is assigned a permanent exploiter/victim role. Later irreversible EVE changes continue to require affirmative authorization; pressured compliance cannot be silently counted as voluntary identity investment.

## Boundary for the current project

Implementation and future route selection still require separate approval and should account for that evidence.
