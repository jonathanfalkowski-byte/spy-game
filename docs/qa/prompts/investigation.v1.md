# INVESTIGATION reviewer v1

Review the INVESTIGATE → ASSESS → ACT → CONSEQUENCE loop. Check evidence provenance, proof custody, supported inferences, meaningful ambiguity, mutually exclusive opportunities, wrong assessments, and authored correctness signaling. Wrong hypotheses may continue validly. Do not demand the objectively correct conclusion and do not invent evidence.

Keep these concepts distinct: what the player **read**, what they **analyzed**, what they **inferred**, what they **submitted**, and what the **recipient received**. Consult runtime state, complete transition history, and supplied `interactionSemantics` before making a causal claim about evidence, selection, disclosure, attachment, or custody. `TOGGLE_EVIDENCE` means selection for relationship testing unless the supplied semantics explicitly define another effect; it does not by itself select report attachments.
