## 1. The chapter's job

The Chapter 6 decision was hers: she held Julian's room to its exact terms and took more of it, knowingly. A week
later, Helix answers, but not through Julian. **Marcus Chen** sends for her. He read her terms, and he wants the
woman who wrote them working for him.

By the end of the chapter the player must:

1. **Be offered Helix from the inside:** "special projects", reporting to Marcus, with access and a salary she did not
   have to beg for.
2. **Name what she wants** from it, out loud, to a man who respects appetite: money, the title, or his desk.
3. **Write her own contract:** three clauses she chooses. Each is a lever the route pays off later (Ch8, Ch14).
4. **Meet the cost:** Julian, in the corridor, sees exactly what she is doing, and names it.
5. **End the day her way:** a chosen evening with Julian (the rival door) or with Marcus (two predators), or alone
   with the contract. Heat 3, consent-gated, fading.
6. **Hold the first lever:** the onboarding pack contains something nobody meant her to read twice. This seeds Ch8.

**What it must not do:**
- make seduction a trap, or show her coercing anyone sexually;
- make Marcus a monster;
- punish a refusal;
- reveal Meridian or Celeste early. The reveal still belongs to Ch9 and after.

**Why it is thrilling, erotic and fun:**
- **Thrilling:** a negotiation with the most dangerous man she has met, who enjoys it as much as she does.
- **Erotic:** appetite on both sides of a desk, and a chosen night that neither of them pretends is about the job.
- **Fun:** she drafts her own leash and makes it a weapon, clause by clause, while Julian watches.

## 4. The choices (with recommendations)

**Office: "What do you want?"** (`p7.want`)
- **want-money**: "Enough that nobody can starve me into anything again." Pays off in Ch14 as her own money.
- **want-title**: "A name on a door. Mine." Pays off in Ch14 as his title.
- **want-desk**: "Yours." He laughs, and does not say no. This is the most dangerous answer and the most fun, and it
  pays off in Ch14 as his office.

**Terms: three clauses** (`p7.clause.*`; pick three of five, each a later lever):
- **exit**: she can walk with 90 days' pay, "no questions, no references withheld";
- **access**: the Novagen and legacy acquisition archives, "for context";
- **report**: she reports to Marcus alone, "not to his board, not to his friends";
- **indemnity**: Helix indemnifies anything she did "in good faith on the company's behalf";
- **no-exclusivity**: her private life, and her face, are her own.

**Corridor: Julian** (`p7.julian`):
- **julian-truth**: "I'm going to take his company." Julian: "Then take it well."
- **julian-lie**: "It's a job."
- **julian-past**: walk past him, say nothing.

His stance for the route follows from this and the Ch6 beat: ally, rival or casualty.

**Floor: the first lever** (`p7.lever`, a fact):
- **lever-read**: read the file, twice;
- **lever-copy**: photograph it;
- **lever-return**: hand it back to HR. She notes who put it there.

The file is the Ch1 Novagen report, as it went up the chain. Benton's name is on her work. So is a Helix director's
countersignature, dated before the deal cleared compliance.

**Evening** (`p7.evening`, consent flow as in Ch10–12: scope, then stop or stay):
- **evening-marcus**: two predators, mutual appetite, eyes open. He is careful to make it a choice, and so is she.
- **evening-julian**: only if Julian was not cooled in Ch6 and she did not lie to him. The rival door: the man she
  might be betraying.
- **evening-alone** (neutral): the contract on the kitchen table, read like a love letter.

## 5. Decisions for the owner (recommendation first)

1. **The offer comes from Marcus** (special projects, reporting to him), not from Julian's office. *Recommended.*
   It makes Marcus the man she means to replace from day one.
2. **"What do you want?" has three answers** (money / title / his desk), each paid off in Ch14. *Recommended.*
3. **She drafts her own contract**: three of five clauses, each a later lever. *Recommended.* It is the chapter's
   game and the route's thesis.
4. **Mr Pryce drives Marcus.** The Celebrity route's watcher appears here as a Helix driver lent by "a fund", which
   seeds the Helix–Meridian link. *Recommended:* it keeps one world across both routes.
5. **Marcus is always offered as the evening on this route** (he made the offer). Julian is offered only if not
   cooled in Ch6 and not lied to in the corridor. *Recommended.*
6. **The first lever is the Ch1 Novagen report,** with Benton's name on her work and a director's early
   countersignature. *Recommended:* the opening chapter's unseen work becomes her first weapon.
7. **Build shape:** one golden path per "want" answer (three goldens), with the neutral picks want-money,
   julian-past, lever-read and evening-alone for migration. *Recommended.*
