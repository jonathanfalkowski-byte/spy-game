# Chapter 6 — The Cage You Choose

**Design only. No Chapter 6 runtime, schema, choices, art binding, AdultSceneSpec handoff, or reader UI is implemented by this document.**

## Core question

Which parts of Evelynn’s new life are still easy to walk away from?

The chapter follows Chapter 3’s choice, Chapter 4’s use of choice, and Chapter 5’s enjoyment of benefit with a narrower problem: a benefit becomes constraining only when repeated reliance, accepted obligation and costly alternatives are shown in sequence.

`BENEFIT → EXPECTATION → FRICTION → REVELATION → CONSTRAINT → COUNTERPOWER`

Glamour, money, femininity, public attention, sex and Julian are never evidence of corruption by themselves. Every constraint needs a named provider, a source, a term, an alternative, and a player response.

## Six movements

| Movement | Working scene purpose | State-sensitive entry | Player power | Exit / reversal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Benefit connected | A current convenience intersects with another actual choice: access, time, presentation, privacy or money. | Provider/resource, self-funded alternative, public artifact. | Ask who benefits; choose an independent workaround; accept a limited favor; use a prior promise as leverage. | The benefit remains useful without silently becoming a debt. |
| 2. Expectation named | A provider, editor, client or institution makes a concrete request tied to a real prior term. | Exact accepted term or public scope; actor knows the source. | Clarify scope, comply narrowly, negotiate consideration, refuse, or redirect the request. | Corrected wording or fallback preserves a feasible path. |
| 3. Friction among people | Maya, Sloane, Julian, a public contact or another selected person reacts only to received facts. | Delivered message/knowledge, public discovery, relationship history. | Share, withhold, ask for a boundary, call in a favor, or stop using a channel. | The other person can retain their own limit; trust is not a meter. |
| 4. Cost of exit | A practical arrangement proves costly to leave because the player has actually used it more than once or accepted a term. | Repeated benefit plus defined obligation and weakened alternative. | Pay the cost, negotiate, use a public/independent option, expose the term, or deepen reliance knowingly. | A recovery path is materially difficult but present. |
| 5. Proof, not confession | The unknown sender is tested. The Rook claim is evaluated through a proof sequence, not accepted by assertion. | Verified-date trail, documents, possible contacts and player-held evidence. | Demand corroboration, compare sources, conceal proof, trade it, disclose it, or walk away. | Lack of proof leaves Rook untrusted; proof creates information, not allegiance. |

## Julian path

Julian remains a 49-year-old Helix COO whose professional help can be real, bounded and valuable. He may care, be attracted where a fresh exchange earns it, have institutional reasons, honor a limit, or react badly to manipulation. None of these are defaulted from access, a coffee, an invitation or a prior intimate outcome.

A Julian constraint exists only when a later request is sourced to an actual professional term, provider benefit, personal agreement or public consequence. The player can accept, narrow, refuse, replace the benefit with self-funding, make a counteroffer or exploit Julian’s own need for the relationship. His possible influence is not evidence that every resource or public path secretly originates with him.

## Independent and non-Julian paths

- A self-funded or public-access player has fewer obligations, more privacy and more direct cost. Elite convenience may be unavailable without becoming a punishment.
- A public/editorial player can choose visibility as a tool: accept selective opportunities, restrict use, bargain over compensation, correct misrepresentation, or use audience attention to protect someone.
- A player with accepted editorial/service terms faces the exact wording, duration, consideration and fallback already established. No new exclusivity is inferred.
- Maya’s response follows actual knowledge and her own boundaries; she may support, challenge, disengage or request truth without becoming an instrument of another path.
- Sloane can correctly identify that external infrastructure has its own risks while remaining limited to sourced information and not becoming automatically right about Evelynn.
- Voss remains bounded by clinical/administrative scope. A qualification or formal review is not a personal relationship or a new procedure.

## Rook proof chain

The reveal proposition is: **Rook was the operative who previously lived and performed the established Evelynn identity.** It is a design target, not an implemented runtime fact.

The player must be able to test it through at least three independent links:

1. **Specific knowledge:** Rook supplies a precise Singapore operational detail that the known verified-date trail makes relevant but does not itself prove.
2. **Controlled artifact:** a dated operational item or older Evelyn record has provenance, limited custody and a contradiction-resistant detail.
3. **External corroboration:** Celeste, Marcus, or another already sourced witness can confirm only what they could plausibly know; they do not reveal private medical history or a complete identity dossier.
4. **Player verification:** Evelynn compares the item with her own retained evidence or forces Rook to predict a fact before disclosure.

One claim, a photograph, a public image, or “trust me” never completes the proof. Corroboration establishes that Rook knew/was involved in a specified past operation; it does not establish motive, loyalty, consent, or the full ORACLE plan.

## ORACLE escalation

After the proof threshold, Rook can point to an authenticated ORACLE assessment: it predicted high voluntary adoption of the Evelynn identity and low long-term Sloane control. The chapter may establish that Sloane knew that limited prediction and proceeded. It must not reveal Sloane’s final motive, establish omniscience, or treat a prediction as causation. That unresolved reason carries into Chapter 7.

## Consent and intimacy boundary

Intimacy remains optional, state-dependent and non-graphic. Any later `AdultSceneSpec` must be triggered by a fresh current choice and record separate eligibility, knowledge, scope, willingness, refusal/withdrawal, aftermath and branch-safe exit. Dependency pressure never substitutes for consent. No Chapter 6 intimate handoff is designed or registered here.

## End state

The climax shows an actual exit cost attached to one previously useful arrangement, paired with enough verified counter-information for Evelynn to act. The player’s end choices must include protecting someone, enforcing a term, refusing a benefit, spending to leave, deepening a relationship knowingly, exposing a system, or turning leverage back on a provider. The dramatic result is increased knowledge and agency, not a verdict that Evelynn is trapped.

## FULL-GAME DESIGN AMENDMENT — NOT CURRENT FROZEN RUNTIME

Chapter 6 can carry route-sensitive heat lanes while keeping the Rook proof chain and counterpower as the core investigation:

- **Executive / Julian lane:** negotiate care, access, benefit, desire, terms, and an exit without treating proximity as consent or a favor as debt.
- **Public / celebrity lane:** negotiate body, image, visibility, rights, and public interpretation without treating appearance as agency.
- **Reputable adult-media professional lane:** a future offer must state real work terms, rights, representation, participants, boundaries, payment, distribution, and exit. Chosen adult work is not a corruption route.
- **Spy lane:** seduction can be an optional tactic or social possibility, but sex is never required for the core case, proof, or mission result.
- **Dependency / exploitation lane:** record the threat, meaningful alternatives, compliance or refusal, harm, evidence, aftermath, and recovery. It remains non-graphic and can never become a willing `AdultSceneSpec` by re-description.

Any later implementation must preserve the [Master GDD](../design/EVE_MASTER_GDD.md) autonomy model, apply fresh authorization to a handoff, and create an independent non-intimate counterplay option.
