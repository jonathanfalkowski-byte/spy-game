## 1. The route in one paragraph

On the Predator road Evelynn **takes** Helix. On the Executive road she is **given** its rooms, by Julian Mercer,
Helix's Group COO: the workroom, then the job, then his life. Julian is the one man in the game who means it when he
says "Tell me what you want, and that's what happens." The route asks what that is worth, and what it costs, in a
world where Meridian holds Helix by a clause in the smallest type and holds Evelynn by a name. Its weapon is
**trust**, which on this road is sharper than leverage and far easier to lose. Its pressure is the **kept**
question: when the flat, the card and the car are his, is she his? The test the owner set for every scene applies:
**thrilling, erotic, fun**.

## 2. Content rules (restated, because this route leans on them)

- **Julian is never a trap.** He is not Meridian's man, not the board member, and not a plant. His care is real
  in every branch, and no consent or intimacy choice is ever revealed as a mistake after the fact (autonomy law).
  He is a decent man inside a machine: as Group COO his signature is on the fund's clause 14.3 pledges that Marcus
  negotiates, and that is how Meridian holds him.
- **Orders target his trust, never his body.** Celeste's orders on this road ask Evelynn for Julian's calendar,
  his signature and his silence. They are non-sexual, and each has a real cost to trust.
- **The kept overlay is shown honestly and never punished.** Dependency on his rooms, his card and his flat is
  something she can choose with open eyes or break, and the route respects both.
- **Heat 3, consent in character** (the scope choice, then stop / stay), fading at the act. The reserved
  sexual-coercion beat stays Ch13 (shared, off screen); Julian's refuge after it is being held, nothing more (canon).

## 5. Endings (the Executive positions, ENDGAME §7)

- **The term enforced:** with Julian, through Helix and Axiom leverage, she strikes clause 14.3 and forces Meridian
  to release Helix. Status kept. The strongest ending with the most allies, and the one that needs trust intact.
- **Exit with rights intact:** she walks out of Helix and Julian's flat with her rights, her name and nothing owed.
  The kept overlay is broken. Julian may come too, as a partner and not a keeper, or not.
- **Status spent:** she spends everything she built to keep Julian standing, and walks away with less and cleaner
  hands.
- **Nell's name:** shared across routes.

## 6. Decisions for the owner (recommendation first)

6. **Ch13 stays the shared placement,** and the Executive choice is what she tells Julian and when. His refuge is
   being held (canon). *Recommended.*
7. **The endings:** the term enforced (with Julian), exit with rights intact (the kept overlay broken), status spent
