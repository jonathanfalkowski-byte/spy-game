# Content direction: Mature, with coercion as the engine

**Owner decision (2026-09-24):** EVE is a **Mature** dark espionage noir erotic thriller,
not an adults-only explicit game. The aim is a wider audience. Blackmail, coercion and
being forced into acts Evelynn doesn't want to do stay central. What changes is how they
are shown.

This document sets the rules every chapter script, art job and build plan is designed
against. It sits beside [CAMPAIGN_PACING_PLAN.md](CAMPAIGN_PACING_PLAN.md) (length and
acts), [CAMPAIGN_ROUTE_MAP.md](CAMPAIGN_ROUTE_MAP.md) (the four lanes and the overlays) and
[ENDGAME_RECONVERGENCE.md](ENDGAME_RECONVERGENCE.md) (Celeste, Meridian, Sloane).

It **supersedes** the explicit-content policy of 2026-09-23 recorded in
[EXPLICIT_SCENE_REGISTER.md](EXPLICIT_SCENE_REGISTER.md) and
[../art/OWNER_EXPLICIT_FRAMES.md](../art/OWNER_EXPLICIT_FRAMES.md).

## 1. The rating target

**Mature 17+ (ESRB M) / PEGI 18.** On Steam this is the main store with the "Some Nudity or
Sexual Content" and "Frequent Violence or Gore / General Mature Content" descriptors. It is
**not** the "Adult Only Sexual Content" category.

Reference point: *Baldur's Gate 3* (M-rated, with nudity and sex scenes, no explicit
close-ups, sold on consoles). Tonal references: *Red Sparrow*, *The Americans*, le Carré.

Why: explicit sexual content combined with non-consent is the combination storefronts and
payment processors pull (the 2025 Steam and itch.io adult-content delistings targeted
exactly this). A Mature game sells on every major store.

## 2. What's on screen and what isn't

| | On screen | Off screen |
|---|---|---|
| **Chosen intimacy** (Julian, Sebastian, Theo, any consensual partner) | Desire, tension, kissing, undressing, sensual framing, partial nudity (bare back, shoulders, legs, silhouettes), bodies in bed before and after | Explicit sex acts, genitals. The scene cuts at the point of sex and picks up after |
| **Coerced sexual acts** | The order arriving; the leverage; her decision; getting ready; the walk to the door; the door closing | **Everything behind the door.** Never shown, never described as it happens, never framed as arousing |
| **Aftermath of coercion** | The drive home, the shower, what she can't say to Maya, the ledger of what it cost, the recovery step | Graphic description of what happened |
| **Violence** | Threat, injury, consequence, the occasional brutal moment | Lingering gore |

Heat scale (from the intimate cards): chosen scenes may run to **heat 3** (sensual,
implied, partial nudity). Heat 4–5 (explicit) is out of the base game.

## 3. The coercion framework

Coercion is the game's engine, a system the player plays against, not imagery.

### 3a. Leverage

Every coercer holds something specific. The game tracks it as **leverage**, visible to the
player, a sourced record like everything else (not a hidden meter):

| Holder | What they hold | What they want | What they'll do if refused |
|---|---|---|---|
| (example) Celeste | Maya's name on a Meridian watch list | Evelynn to plant a file in Julian's workroom | Have Maya's clearance quietly revoked; then worse |

A **leverage board** (a phone or dossier screen in the fiction) shows who holds what, what
they have asked for, and what they have threatened. It's where the player plans a way out.

### 3b. Orders, and the three answers

Every coercion beat is an **order** with three answers, each with a real cost:

1. **Comply.** She does it. The leverage holds, the holder gets bolder, and it's recorded
   (it can come back).
2. **Refuse.** She won't. The threat lands on what's held: Maya's job, housing, safety,
   exposure. **Refusal never triggers sexual punishment of Evelynn.** The cost is real, but
   it's the threat that was named.
3. **Counterplay.** She finds a third way. This needs something she's built: evidence,
   an ally, an audience, a lever of her own. It's harder and slower, and it's how the
   player takes the leverage away.

No order is a trap with one correct answer. The player carries the choice.

### 3c. What the orders are

Most coerced acts are **spy work she hates**: lying to Maya, planting evidence, betraying
someone who trusted her, stealing, informing, walking into a room she knows is a setup.

**Sexual coercion is rare, reserved and heavy.** At most one or two beats in a whole
playthrough (for example, a honeypot assignment Meridian or Celeste demands in Act III).
When it happens, it follows §2: the order, the choice, the door closing, the aftermath. It
uses the existing **exploitation / recovery overlay**
([CAMPAIGN_ROUTE_MAP.md](CAMPAIGN_ROUTE_MAP.md)): a named threat, a constrained
alternative, harm, then a recovery step. It is never the default outcome of a refusal,
never a reward, and never a corruption path the game treats as sexy.

### 3d. Who can hurt Maya, and how

The threat to Maya is the heart of the blackmail. It is shown through **non-sexual harm**:
her job, her clearance, her housing, her reputation, surveillance, fear, physical danger
in a thriller sense. Maya is never threatened sexually.

## 4. The erotic charge

The erotic core of the game lives in **what Evelynn chooses**:

- Julian, Sebastian and Theo (and any later consensual partner) stay **sensual and on screen**
  up to heat 3: charged, adult, sometimes dangerous, always chosen.
- The contrast between what she chooses and what is taken from her is the game's
  emotional spine. The chosen scenes should feel like hers. The coerced ones should feel
  like a cost.
- Partners stay **mostly men** (owner direction 2026-09-23). Evelynn is always styled:
  hair done, heels, fitted, finished (style lock).

## 5. Hard lines (unchanged, restated)

- The clinic and the identity treatment are never sexualized.
- No sexual content involving anyone who is or reads as a minor.
- Non-consent is never eroticized: no arousal framing, no lingering, no "she secretly
  wanted it".
- Consent gates on chosen scenes stay (the existing invitation, uncertainty, refusal and
  withdrawal beats).
- Intimacy is never required to solve the investigation (autonomy law).

## 6. Player comfort (recommended for reach)

- **Content notice at the start** naming the themes: blackmail, coercion including sexual
  coercion (implied, off screen), violence, identity manipulation.
- **An optional "fade the coercion beats" setting** that shortens the lead-in to a coerced
  scene to a single line, keeping the choice and the cost. The story is unchanged.

## 7. Art

- **Sensual, never explicit.** Chosen scenes: silhouettes, bare backs and shoulders,
  lingerie, bodies in bed before and after, hands, the moment before. No genitals, no
  explicit acts, no nipples in the base game.
- **Coercion scenes are shown by place and aftermath:** the corridor, the door, the car
  afterwards, the bathroom mirror. Never the act.
- Everything stays in the house style: dark noir, inside the luminance band
  ([../art/EVE_STYLE_LOCK.md](../art/EVE_STYLE_LOCK.md)).

## 8. What this changes in the project

- **Nothing shipped needs pulling.** The game as built is already Mature-compliant:
  Sebastian's sex path is switched off (the no-sex body ships), and Julian's Chapter 4 and 5
  sex scopes fade to black.
- **The explicit-scene register** becomes a register of **fade and heat-3 points**. Its
  DEFERRED explicit bodies are closed as "out of scope for the base game". The Sebastian
  sex path either stays switched off or is rewritten as a heat-3 fade version.
- **The owner explicit-frames workflow** and the local explicit-writer pipeline are
  **shelved**. They are kept on disk in case an optional patch is ever made (§9).
- **Art:** no explicit CGs in the base game. Intimate CGs are heat 3 at most.

## 9. Optional 18+ patch (deferred)

Undecided and not planned. If it is ever made:

- **Consensual scenes only.** Coerced content never goes in the patch.
- Distributed separately from the store build, as many visual novels do.
- The base game must be complete and satisfying without it.

## 10. Where the blackmail lives in the story

**Act III, the Counteroffensive (~Ch10–15)** is the blackmail arc
([CAMPAIGN_PACING_PLAN.md](CAMPAIGN_PACING_PLAN.md)). At the midpoint (Ch9) Evelynn finds
Celeste's name. In Act III, Celeste realises she has been found, and uses what Meridian
holds (Maya, Adrian's real identity, Evelynn's housing, the ledger leaf) to turn Evelynn
into an asset again. The act is a sequence of orders escalating in cost, the leverage board
filling up, and the player working toward the counterplay that breaks it. Its route
flavour follows the lane (institutional, outside, executive, own-power). Pressure can
start earlier. Sloane's monitoring and Rook's price are already leverage in Acts I–II.

## 11. Checklist for every scene script

1. Is anything sexual on screen chosen and at heat 3 or below?
2. If anything sexual is coerced: is it off screen, with the order, the choice and the
   aftermath on screen, under the exploitation/recovery overlay?
3. Does every coercion beat offer comply, refuse and counterplay, each with a real cost?
4. Does any refusal lead to sexual punishment? (It must not.)
5. Is the threat to Maya non-sexual?
6. Is the leverage specific and recorded?
7. Is the clinic untouched by sexual content?

## Decisions (owner, 2026-09-24): all settled as recommended

1. **Rating:** Mature 17+ / PEGI 18.
2. **Sexual coercion frequency:** at most one or two beats per playthrough, always off
   screen.
3. **Player comfort:** a content notice at the start plus the optional "fade the coercion
   beats" setting. **Built 2026-09-25 with Chapter 13**: the chapter-open notice, and "Fade coercion
   scenes" in Settings (a per-reader preference outside the save; `fadeCoercion13`).
4. **Sebastian's switched-off sex path:** rewrite it as a heat-3 fade, so the choice exists
   and the scene fades like Julian's. **Done 2026-09-24** (`src/content/chapter5-sebastian.ts`).
5. **The 18+ patch:** undecided, deferred.
