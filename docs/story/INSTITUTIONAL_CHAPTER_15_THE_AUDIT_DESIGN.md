## 1. The chapter's job

On the Executive road she walks into the archive by appointment. On this road she walks in **by audit**: Axiom is a
client of long standing, clients may audit what they bought, and the contract Sloane signed three years ago has an
audit clause nobody at the Vesper has ever read. The drawer that matters on this road is **Axiom's client file**:
every receipt, E.V. (II), and the one for **CANDIDATE 9C, Priya**, still undelivered.

By the end of the chapter the player must:

1. **Choose the crew** (one or two): **Sloane** (if allied: "Backup. Mine."), **Daniel** (if he knows), **Iris** (if
   free), **Maya** (if restored), **Marsh** outside with the switch (if an ally), or nobody.
2. **Go in, by the way Ch14 left her:**
   - **audit** (`formal`): Tuesday at ten, Axiom's audit on paper under clause 22 of its contract; Sloane in a van
     outside; the assistant brings coffee;
   - **notice** (`regulator`, only with Marsh an ally): a Markets Authority notice to produce, Marsh at her side with
     his lanyard and his bicycle clips;
   - **escort** (`benton`): Axiom's authority, Benton's version: Benton walks the audit with her, as Meridian's man,
     and she has to work round him;
   - **stair** (Iris free, or Sloane's keys): 2 a.m.;
   - **invited** (**always**): a meeting she asks Celeste for "to discuss the board", as cover.
3. **Survive the snag:** talk / hide / bold.
4. **Take what is hers:** her page (always); **Axiom's client file** (always), with Priya's receipt; and **one thing
   more:** Adrian's file (defusing the name if she spent it in Ch14), the 1109 safe (if the card isn't already out), or
   Nell's Jakarta order.
5. **Priya's receipt:** **tear it up** (9C is never delivered), **keep it** (evidence), or **give it to Priya** (the
   hardest honest thing).
6. **Break the holds** in the week after (Maya's warning withdrawn; Adrian's name defused; copies to the switch), and
   **pay one cost:** **ally** (Iris's cover, or Marsh early), **badge** (she resigns her Axiom commission: no rank, no
   protection), **money**, or **Sloane** (if allied: Sloane goes on the record and loses her directorate, by her own
   choice).
7. **"No more orders."** "I shall be there as myself." The phone: return / river / keep. The night: Daniel (if he
   knows; heat 3, consent-gated, fades), a partner from before, or alone.
8. **Pin the card:** THE BOARD MEETS. Act IV (Institutional) is in development until its chapters exist.

**What it must not do:** make Sloane a romance; put anything sexual on screen that isn't chosen; topple Meridian, or
resolve Nell's death (Act IV).
