## 1. The chapter's job

The Institutional road began with a woman walking back into the building that made her, sitting down at her own desk,
and writing the scope of what they could ask of her. Over it hung a question in pencil: **WHO IS WATCHING HER?**, about
the handler who was, it turned out, on a leash of her own.

It ends with a case file closed the way institutions close them: **NO FURTHER ACTION**. And with that question
answered, in her hand, next to a second card she writes for herself: **WHO IS WATCHING ME?**

By the end of the chapter the player must:

1. **See what the board bought:** the morning after, by `act4.board`, with Celeste's last word (canon) and Axiom's.
2. **Hold her position**, by aim and terms (§4), and see **where Sloane ends** (§4). Then **the switch**: armed,
   handed on, or disarmed.
3. **Deal with the green light** in the hall.
4. **Walk Axiom's floor** one last time, or for the first time as herself: Terry, Daniel, Priya, Maya, the desk by the
   pillar. If Daniel still doesn't know, **she can tell him now.** Then who she goes home to: Daniel (if he knows), a
   partner from before, Maya, or nobody.
5. **Answer who she is now:** Adrian, Evelyn, or a new name, in the NAME box of whatever file is hers.
6. **Close a year later on a page headed SCOPE:** three terms for a life, not a job, and Sloane's pencil in the margin
   if Sloane is still in it. A chosen last night or a quiet one. Then the two cards, and the last line.

**What it must not do:**
- make Sloane a romance, or a reward; her ending is a person in the machine, placed by what Evelynn made her;
- make any ending a punishment: walking out unprotected is freedom, and the chapter says so; being known is not being
  owned;
- put the green light anywhere near the last night (monitoring is never sexualised; the light is dealt with first, in
  daylight, by itself);
- make Daniel a partner who was never told;
- topple Meridian, or contradict the canon (Celeste in Lisbon; Meridian wounded and standing).

**Why it is thrilling, erotic and fun:**
- **Thrilling:** holding a switch that could still end Meridian, and filing it, calmly, under NO FURTHER ACTION; being
  the one person in the building everybody knows the truth about, and walking across the floor anyway.
- **Erotic:** a year on, a man who said hello to a stranger saying hello to her by her own name, and a night that is
  chosen, not tasked.
- **Fun:** filling in Axiom's own form to have Axiom's camera removed. Terry. Sloane's pencil in the margin, one last
  time.

## 4. The positions, Sloane, Daniel, the page and the cards (with recommendations)

**The position** (`end.position`), by `act4.aim` and `act4.terms`:
- **inside:**
  - **full:** the Project Eve contract terminated for defect; 9C never delivered; Axiom indemnified. She keeps
    Axiom's protection and takes a rank of her own: **Officer, Client Assurance**, with one job: read every contract
    Axiom signs, and strike any clause that buys a person. A desk by the window on seventy-one. **Known:** the whole
    floor knows what she was, and the board has decided she is worth more than the scandal. With `cost15 = badge`,
    Terry gives her the badge back across the gate, and it has a new number;
  - **partial:** the contract suspended, 9C "deferred", her rank "acting"; the rest is a year of Maya's inquiry and the
    switch behind her;
  - **none:** Axiom terminates alone and slowly, a clause at a time. Solvable, costlier.
- **channels:**
  - **full:** Meridian referred to the regulator by its own board, first; Maya's findings published; Axiom survives
    as the client that reported. She keeps Axiom's protection and loses her rank: a witness, not an officer, and she
    finds she prefers it;
  - **partial:** the findings filed, not published; the regulator slow; her protection kept;
  - **none:** she files it herself, through Marsh, the long way.
- **walk:**
  - **full:** the undertaking signed: her name never placed, catalogued or sold again. She hands in her badge (or
    Terry already has it), and walks out of the staff gate with the file in her head, unprotected and free;
  - **partial:** without the signatures. She doesn't need them;
  - **none:** she walks anyway. It was always the one thing in her scope nobody could take.
- **nell:** the minute records Eleanor Linden (if said), and Holland Village with Nora, and the harbour wall at dusk
  (shared canon).

**Where Sloane ends** (`end.sloane`), by what Evelynn made her (§1 of the route design):
- **promoted** (vouched for in Ch17, or allied with the inside aim granted): Director of a directorate that didn't
  exist last month. "They've given me a directorate for reading contracts. I've read every one of them twice. You'd
  be amazed what we've bought.";
- **holding the pen** (channels, or `cost15 = sloane`, or let stand): she writes the report, the one that saved her,
  and signs it as the officer of record. "It's the best thing I've ever written. Nobody will read it. That's how you
  know it worked.";
- **reassigned** (used as the proof, in Ch14 or Ch17): Records, aisle nine, at her own request. "Somebody should know
  where everything is." Not a punishment: the one room in the building where nobody watches her;
- **retired** (cut loose in Ch14): a flat in Pimlico with a view of nothing, and a postcard with no picture on it:
  NO FURTHER ACTION. V.

**Daniel's hello** (`end.daniel`), on the floor:
- **if he knows:** he looks up from his desk, and says it the way he said it to a stranger in Ch7, on purpose: "Hi.
  Daniel. I sit there, I mostly eat there." And then her name, whichever one she has chosen to give him. It is the
  road's first line answered;
- **if he doesn't:** he says hello to a stranger again, kindly, and she has a choice she has never had on a Monday:
  **tell-daniel** (she tells him, at his desk, all of it; he is quiet for a long time, and then: "I knew the coffee
  machine. I didn't know I knew you." It sets `inst.daniel-told`; he is not a partner tonight, and may be a year later)
  / **let-it-be** (he never knows, and is never deceived into anything).

**The page** (`end.scope`), a year later: headed **SCOPE**, as in Ch7, but for a life. She chooses three from:
- **refusal:** "I may decline anything, once, in writing, and it stays declined.";
- **record:** "I see my own file. All of it. Every month.";
- **name:** "Nobody is tasked against me by means of who I was.";
- **backup:** "Every hard thing has a number that answers.";
- **people:** "Nobody I love is ever a means to anything.";
- **leave** (new): "I may leave, at any time, and it will not be called desertion."

Sloane's pencil, if she is in Evelynn's life (promoted, holding the pen, or reassigned and still writing): one line in
the margin by each, the Ch7 margin answered: backup, "Mine. Always mine." becomes **"Yours now."**; refusal, "Once per
tasking. Not once per career." becomes **"As often as you like."**; people, "Define 'loves'." becomes **"I'll take
it as written. I always did."** Retired, she sends it back from Pimlico, pencilled anyway: "I can't sign this. I've
pencilled it anyway."

**The two cards** (`end.cards`), on the wardrobe door:
- **WHO IS WATCHING HER?**, answered in ink by where Sloane ended:
  - promoted or holding the pen: **I AM.** (attention and power, never romance: the one person who knows the machine as
    well as she does, keeping an eye on the other);
  - reassigned: **SHE IS. SHE ALWAYS WAS.**
  - retired: **NOBODY. GOOD.**
- **WHO IS WATCHING ME?**, her own, answered by the light: **NOBODY. THE LIGHT IS OFF.** (down or form) / **NOBODY. I
  KEPT THE TAPE.** (tape) / **NOBODY. I CHECKED.** (the new flat).

**The last line**, by name:
- **Adrian:** *My name is Adrian Vale. I sat down at my own desk and nobody stopped me. This time, nobody had to.*
- **Evelyn:** *My name is Evelyn Vale. I was delivered with a file number. I wrote my own scope, and it held.*
- **New:** *The light in the hall is off. Nobody is watching. I wrote my name down anyway.*
