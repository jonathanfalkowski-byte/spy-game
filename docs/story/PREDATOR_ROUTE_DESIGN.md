# The Predator route: design (for owner approval)

**Status: APPROVED (owner, 2026-09-27: all six decisions as recommended).** The lane split (§3) is **built**: the other chapters
each get their own design doc before build. Built so far, and continuous: Ch7 (The Offer), Ch8 (The Floor), Ch9 (shared),
Ch10 (Let Me Help), Ch11 (The Catalogue), Ch12 (The Counterparty), Ch13 (The Mirror), Ch14 (Marcus Falls), Ch15
(The Key) and Ch16 (The Seventh Chair). Still to come: the Ch17–18 variants. Written 2026-09-27 as the next route after Celebrity (CAMPAIGN_ROUTE_MAP decision 7).

## 1. The route in one paragraph

On the Celebrity road the world wants to own Evelynn, and she learns to own herself. On the **Predator** road she
decides to own **Helix**. She walks into Marcus Chen's company with the one thing everyone underestimated: she
remembers every word of every room. She takes Helix from the inside with **leverage, secrets and charm**, climbing
over Marcus and around Julian toward the Helix–Meridian counterparty where the board-member reveal waits. It is
the dark mirror of the Celebrity Act III. There she is blackmailed; here **she holds the leverage**, and the route
asks what that does to her. The test the owner set for every scene applies: **thrilling, erotic, fun**.

## 2. Content rules (unchanged, restated because this route leans hardest on them)

- **She never sexually coerces anyone.** Her weapons are secrets, leverage, timing and charm. Seduction on this
  route is chosen by both people, knowingly: two predators who like the game. It is never a trap sprung through
  sex.
- **Heat 3, consent in character** (scope choice, then stop / stay), fade at the act. Partners are men.
- **Coercion on screen is hers, and it is non-sexual:** a document, a ledger, a career, a secret. Every lever is
  one the player earned, sourced in the save.
- **The route has a moral cost.** People she levers lose things. The game shows the cost without lecturing, and
  lets her be magnificent and a little frightening.

## 5. Chapter sketches (enough to approve; each gets its own design doc before build)

**Ch7 · The Offer.** Marcus's corner office at nine in the morning. He has read her Ch6 terms and admires them. Moments:
- how she answers "What do you want?" (money / the title / "your desk");
- what she writes into the contract, which the player drafts line by line;
- Julian in the corridor after: "He'll eat you." "He'll try."

The evening is optional: a chosen evening with **Julian** (the rival door), or with **Marcus** (two predators). It
is heat 3, consent-gated, and fades. The Marcus evening is written as mutual appetite, not a transaction.

**Ch8 · The Floor.** Three weeks inside Helix, played as a hub of *levers*, like the Ch9 hub:
- a director's padded expenses;
- a general counsel's affair (she learns it; the game lets her **not** use it);
- the Novagen file from Ch1, re-read from the inside (Benton, Axiom and Helix all in one document).

Each lever has a price and a person on the other end. Mr Pryce drives Marcus. The first Meridian name appears in a
Helix wire.

**Ch12 · The Counterparty.** Geneva in the rain: the Helix–Meridian fund's private bank, a banker called Lucien
Morel who is the only man in the game as good at this as she is, and a heat-3 evening that is mutual and dangerous.
Nell's trail runs from the money side: nine flats, nine accounts, one signature, C. It replaces Singapore on this
route, and Nora is reached by phone in Ch14.

**Ch13 · The Mirror.** The honeypot inverted, and the route's moral centre. Meridian asks the client's new favourite
to *run* a placement against Owen Marsh. The player sees what Evelynn became on the other road, from the operator's
chair.
- Comply is off screen, cut at the briefing. The cost lands on Evelynn: the placed woman's face at the Vesper later.
- Refuse costs her standing at Helix.
- Counter: she turns Marsh and blows the placement to Meridian's discredit.

The content rules hold: nothing sexual is shown or described.

**Ch14 · Marcus Falls.** The payoff of every lever. How she takes him:
- the board;
- the press (a Celebrity crossover);
- a quiet resignation letter he signs in her presence.

Julian's stance is set by the player's Ch7–8 choices: ally, rival or casualty.

**Ch16–18.** The board scenes are shared; she enters as a client's representative. The positions are Predator's
own:
- **the seat accepted** (she becomes Celeste, eyes open; the darkest ending);
- **the seat refused** and Meridian wounded from inside Helix;
- **walking away with Helix**, Meridian kept at arm's length.

## 8. Decisions for the owner (recommendation first)

1. **Lane split:** carve Predator out of Executive with the §3 seeds. *Recommended.* The alternative is a Ch7
   player-choice split only.
2. **Ch12 location:** Geneva and the money trail, not Singapore. *Recommended:* it keeps Predator distinct, and
   Singapore stays Celebrity's emotional heart.
3. **Ch13 "The Mirror":** she is asked to run a placement, with the comply outcome off screen, as above.
   *Recommended*, and the route's moral centre. Or soften it to her being asked only to *recruit*.
4. **Marcus as a chosen partner** (mutual appetite, heat 3). *Recommended yes*; it fits "partners mostly men" and
   the route's erotic charge.
5. **The board seat is acceptable on Predator** (the darkest ending). *Recommended yes*: an ending is a position,
   and this is the position the route earns.
6. **Build order inside the route:** Ch7, 8, then 13–14 (the unique spine), then the shared variants, then 12.
   *Recommended.*
