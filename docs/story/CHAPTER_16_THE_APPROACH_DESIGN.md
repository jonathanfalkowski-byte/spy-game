## 1. The chapter's job

Chapter 15 ended on Wednesday night with one card left on Celeste's side of the wall: *THE BOARD MEETS*, and one
word: *Tomorrow.* Chapter 16 is **Thursday, from first light to the Vesper's door**: the approach, in the
endgame's own vocabulary (ENDGAME §7). Chapter 17 is the room.

By the end of the chapter the player must:

1. **Know what she holds**: the case reviewed one last time from what Act III actually left (evidence in custody,
   the ORACLE verdict, witnesses, exposure, allies, money), as a single honest strength.
2. **Choose what she wants to walk out of the room with**, the **aim**, which Chapter 18 turns into her position:
   the truth in public, terms nobody can revoke, Nell's name said out loud, or a clean way out.
3. **Choose who comes** (up to two inside the room, one outside) and **what she brings** (the order things go on
   the table, and one card held back).
4. **Arrive**, in a way that reacts to her exposure: the operation sees her coming, or it doesn't (ENDGAME §7).
5. **Walk through the Vesper's door** into the long room, where the board sits under the empty frames, and
   Celeste stands up "as herself". Chapter 17 begins in that room.

What it must not do: hold the confrontation (Ch17) or the ending (Ch18); rewrite what she did (logistics, not
biography); gate the name or the action behind any ally or intimacy (autonomy law: the free-agent core alone can
still walk in and act; it only costs more).

**Why it is thrilling, erotic and fun:** the thrill is a heist-planner's morning with the stakes of a trial, and a
walk through a door she has walked through as property; the erotic charge is chosen and quiet (getting dressed
for it as power, a partner's hands on a clasp at the back of her neck, if she wants them there); the fun is
choosing the order of the cards, and the one she keeps in her pocket.

## 4. Scene by scene

### 4.1 Thursday (`dawn`)

Five in the morning. The wall (or the wardrobe door). She takes every card down, one by one, and lays them on the
floor in the order they will matter, the way Adrian laid out a filing before a hearing. What each is, where it came
from, whether it would stand up. The case, stated honestly, as a card of its own: `act4.case`, with its reasons
(the signed verdict counts for most; Nell's order; the 1109 safe; a witness who was there; the switch).

The black phone, if she kept it, silent in its freezer bag. Celeste's last message on the screen of her memory:
*I shall be there as myself.*

### 4.2 What She Wants (`aim`)

The real decision of the chapter, made at the kitchen table with the cards on the floor. **The aim**
(`act4.aim`), which Chapter 18 turns into a position (ENDGAME §7, own-power: "hold the proof no one can revoke;
audience as shield"):
- **aim-expose** · the truth in public: Meridian named, the defect on every screen, Celeste's signature in the
  papers. The audience as shield. It costs her the last of her privacy (or spends what `cost = visibility` began).
- **aim-terms** · terms nobody can revoke: Maya safe for good, Adrian's name retired, Sloane cleared, the
  catalogue closed to her forever, and the switch kept armed so it stays that way. Meridian wounded, quietly.
- **aim-nell** · Nell's name said out loud, in that room, by the woman who signed the order: not justice, which
  nobody can give her, but the truth, in front of witnesses. (Nora in the room, if she came.)
- **aim-out** · a clean way out: walk out of the room with the leverage, and out of the life, and be nobody's
  asset, anywhere. Needs money or a door (`cost ≠ money`, or an ally with a boat, or Julian's plane).

### 4.3 Who Comes (`crew`)

Up to **two inside** the room (`act4.inside`) and **one outside** (`act4.outside`), from who is still standing:
- **Inside:** Sloane (Axiom's officer of record; the verdict is hers too), Nora (Nell's sister, if an ally),
  Marsh (the Authority, if turned and not spent), Maya (if she stayed), Iris (if free and not spent), Julian (as
  Helix, a Meridian client with a seat at the table, if in play and not spent). Or **nobody**: the free-agent core,
  walking in alone (autonomy law: allowed, harder).
- **Outside:** Theo (cameras on the embankment), Pryce (the car at the kerb, engine running), the switch holders
  (a phone call at seven if she hasn't walked out), or Maya at the café across the road.

Short scenes, one per person asked: Sloane ironing a shirt she hasn't worn in a year; Nora off the overnight flight
from Singapore with Nell's photograph in her handbag; Marsh with a box of files and his bicycle clips.

### 4.4 The Order of Things (`table`)

What goes on the table first (`act4.first`): the signed verdict (the defect), Nell's order (the burn), the 1109
cards (the product in use), or page seven (herself). And **the card held back** (`act4.held`): one item she keeps
in her pocket for the moment Celeste thinks she has won. Chapter 17 plays it.

### 4.5 Armour (`dress`)

Getting dressed as power, not display (style lock: finished, fitted, heels). **What she wears** (`act4.wear`): the
green (the colour Celeste chose for her, worn as her own now), the black (the colour Celeste would never have dared
put her in), or the grey silk (Iris's colour: the legends' colour). Optional, chosen, quiet: a partner in play
fastening a clasp at the back of her neck, his mouth near her ear, "come back"; or Maya doing her hair at the
bathroom mirror the way she did before Adrian's first hearing. Heat 1–2; nothing more on the morning of the board.

### 4.6 The Embankment (`arrive`)

**The way in** (`act4.arrive`), and the operation reacts to her exposure (ENDGAME §7):
- **arrive-front** · the front door, through whatever is on the embankment. If she is public (`c5.published`,
  `act3.exposed`, `cost = visibility`), there are photographers, and the doorman cannot turn her away in front of
  them: the audience as shield. If not, the embankment is empty, and the door simply opens.
- **arrive-quiet** · the service door in the alley. If she is public, Meridian expects her quiet, and there are two
  men on the service stair; if not, nobody is there at all.
- **arrive-car** · the car Celeste sends at half past five (Pryce driving, if known): arriving as a guest, which is
  what Celeste expects, and the one thing she will not expect her to use.

The doorman: "They're expecting you." Then, for the first time, he adds: "Good luck, Ms Vale."

### 4.7 The Long Room (`complete`)

The long room reset for the board: the empty frames on the walls, a long table under them, six people at it.
Celeste at the head, in black, no jewellery, who stands when Evelynn comes in. Two of the other five she knows
from the verdict's signatures. **Chapter 17 begins here.**

**Last line:** *She stood up when I came in. She has never once stood up for me before.*

## 10. Decisions for the owner (recommendations first)

1. **Chapter 16 is Thursday itself,** dawn to the door, not a further week of planning. *Recommended: yes*:
   Chapter 15 ended on "Tomorrow", and a single day keeps the tension.
2. **The aim is chosen here** (expose, terms, Nell's name, or out) and becomes the ending's position in Ch18.
   *Recommended: yes*: the ending is a position the player chose on the morning, not a verdict handed down.
3. **Up to two inside the room and one outside,** from who is still standing; Nora can be in the room; Julian
   can sit at the table as a Meridian client. Or nobody, which is allowed and harder. *Recommended: yes.*
4. **One card held back** for Chapter 17 to play at the moment Celeste thinks she has won. *Recommended: yes*:
   the fun of the endgame.
5. **The arrival reacts to exposure:** public, and the street is a shield at the front and a trap at the back;
   private, and nobody is waiting. *Recommended: yes* (ENDGAME §7).
6. **The board's other two signatories are named in Ch16:** Anton Deverell (the chair, old money, shipping and
   insurance) and Marguerite Soames (the one who reads everything). *Recommended: yes*; names are placeholders if
   you have others.
7. **A quiet chosen moment while she dresses** (a partner at the clasp, or Maya at her hair), heat 1–2 at most.
   *Recommended: yes*: the morning of the board is armour, not a love scene.
