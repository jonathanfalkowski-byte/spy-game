## Pacing and authority

**Escalate situations quickly. Escalate permanent loss of autonomy gradually.** Each major chapter should aim for an information problem, a character/power confrontation, a memorable dark situation, a prior-behavior consequence and an opportunity carrying a cost. These may intersect; they need not be five isolated scenes. Vary the form rather than repeatedly offering seduction.

Darkness is not an intimacy requirement. Voluntary indulgence can be transgressive; pressure, manipulation, refusal and coercion remain separate authored circumstances. Neither inequality nor a wardrobe suggestion alone establishes coercion. Later irreversible EVE stages still require affirmative authorization; this roadmap proposes no exception. Pressure must never be counted silently as voluntary identity investment.

## State and information discipline

Use existing consequence and information architecture as the future implementation foundation. No automatic generic “darkness,” corruption, dependency or ambition meter follows from this map.

- **Potential leverage:** a holder has sourced material relevant to a subject, with bounded validity and significance. The subject need not know.
- **Disclosed leverage:** a specified recipient is shown or told about that material.
- **Threatened leverage:** a specific demand is tied to a stated consequence. Record the words/source, not just a hostile tone.
- **Exercised leverage:** an actual disclosure, action or resulting compliance occurs. Record what succeeded, failed or remains uncertain.
- **Obligation:** an offer and accepted terms, parties, scope, consideration, fulfillment and remaining commitments. An actor's belief that a favor is owed is a separate attributed belief. Accepting help can have consequences without creating an unlimited debt.
- **Compromise/vulnerability:** identify the act, affected person, evidence, significance and actual recipients. A public photograph, a profession or femininity is not intrinsically compromising. Risk depends on context and use.
- **Publication:** preserve specific permission separately from actual release. The visible artifact can persist and be discovered later; it does not automatically update every NPC. Recognition of Evelynn does not authenticate the Adrian/persona association.
- **Agency:** actual willingness, authorization, participation, refusal, withdrawal and pressure remain separate. Later uses of an asset or later encounters need their own authored basis.

These are design requirements, not declarations that all such writable fields already exist. Exact rule/source bindings require separately approved implementation. An external prose writer cannot create these outcomes.

## Character safeguards and rejected shortcuts

Sloane knows only what a specific report delivers. Rook's authentic information can still be selective; no faction is the guaranteed truth route. Maya retains work, preferences and the ability to refuse help; only a player-created information path makes her relationship or knowledge available as leverage. The residential timestamp alone says nothing about Maya. Voss's medical limits must not turn her into a second intelligence handler.

The proposed male executive may become a credible alternative to Axiom; he may not recite private housing/medical/employment facts unless the player discloses them or a later approved source supplies them. His respected negotiation in Chapter 3 is real, not secretly a forced trap. Later influence over appearance or schedule is a separate request, not included in photo authorization.

Reject: gratuitous public degradation after refusal; secret photography to nullify negotiated terms; universal knowledge from a public image; missing evidence invented for a weak route; Maya threatened merely because she matters privately; intimacy made the price of medical records; instant total dependency after a gift; automatic sexualization of Voss, Rook, Sloane or the executive. All three main power experiences work without sexual content.
