## Pacing guardrails

- Target 2–4 heat beats per substantial chapter after Chapter 3; target 0–2 handoffs only where the chapter actually earns them.
- A handoff cannot deliver mandatory proof, replace a boundary scene, or resolve coercion.
- A non-intimate alternative must be dramatic and mechanically meaningful, not a failure state.
- Every aftermath returns to evidence, terms, relationships, custody, public consequence, recovery, or a playable decision.
