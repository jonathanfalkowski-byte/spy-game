# EVE — Adult Thriller Pacing Map

**Design authority:** EVE Master GDD  
**Scope:** Chapters 4–6 design review  
**Status:** Full-game design amendment; does not silently rewrite frozen runtime revisions.

EVE is an adult spy thriller / life game. Adult tension should recur through attraction, presentation, public exposure, power, nightlife, intimacy, body/image negotiation, dependency, or manipulation. Actual intimate handoffs remain state-gated and non-graphic in canonical runtime.

## Pacing rules

1. Do not stack every adult beat into one route.
2. Do not make Julian the only source of attraction, adult life, or relationship power.
3. Do not require intimacy to obtain core evidence.
4. Do not make every offer threatening; pleasure and opportunity must be genuinely attractive.
5. Do not make every dark event sexual.
6. If coercion occurs, track it as coercion; do not convert it into willing intimacy because Evelynn later enjoys some consequence.
7. Adult beats should alter at least one of: relationship, identity, public image, access, evidence, leverage, resources, obligation, body state, or exposure.
8. Public/adult-career routes need professional terms, rights, compensation, scope, and exit conditions.
9. Body changes need authorship history, not just visual state.
10. Adult content should intensify the espionage/life game rather than interrupt it.
