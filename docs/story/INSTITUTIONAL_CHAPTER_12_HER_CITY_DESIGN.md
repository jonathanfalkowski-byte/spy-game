## 1. The chapter's job

On the Executive road she goes to Singapore on Julian's arm. On this road she goes **on paper**: an Axiom tasking in
Sloane's capitals, SITE SG/EH-9 · ESTABLISH WHAT BECAME OF E.V. (I) · BACKUP: V.S. · THE FULLERTON, ROOM 811. Axiom's
rules put handler and operative on different flights. Everything she learns, she is supposed to write down. The chapter
asks how much of a dead woman she hands to the machine that bought her face.

By the end of the chapter the player must:

1. **Arrive on the books or off them:** Axiom's car from the desk, a taxi paid in cash, or the MRT.
2. **Meet Mrs Tan** (be Evie / the truth / let her decide), and take the key from under the orchids.
3. **Search Number 9** (the bureau / the wardrobe / the balcony), and meet the caretaker: hide, **show the Axiom warrant
   card** ("Axiom. Routine inspection." It works, horribly well), or "Who pays you?".
4. **Meet Ashby** at the Punkah Bar (be Nell / lay it on the bar / "I'm the reissue"): Jakarta, "a friend of hers", the
   orchids. And Axiom: "They bought the reissue from us. The original was never theirs."
5. **Meet Nora** (the truth / the kind lie / the wrong house).
6. **Write the report,** in room 811, with Sloane across the desk: **all of it** (Ashby, Nora, the Sunday-morning call;
   Sloane goes grey at "the tall friend with the beautiful voice"), **shaded** (the site and Ashby, not Nora: "her people"
   in all but name), or **the site only** (Number 9, and nothing else; she keeps the rest).
7. **Stand on the harbour wall** at midnight: say her name / the orchid / stand there (and, if Daniel knows, ring him).
8. **Pin the card:** NELL. ELEANOR LINDEN. And under it the report: ALL / SHADED / THE SITE ONLY.

**What it must not do:** prove Celeste's guilt (Act IV); make Sloane a romance; put anything sexual on screen.

**Why it is thrilling, erotic and fun:** the thrill is a dead woman's flat with a live caretaker at the door and a warrant
card in her pocket; the charge is Sloane in a hotel room at midnight with her shoes off, reading about the first one; the
fun is Mrs Tan.
