## 0. Who the sender is (the one new canon this route needs)

**The sender is "R." on the ledger leaf: Nell's courier on Meridian's Singapore line, and the man she was leaving
with.** *Recommended.*

- **Name:** Rafe Lim. Forty-one, Singapore-born, ten years a courier for Meridian's Singapore station under Colin
  Ashby: documents, drives, handoffs at two in the morning. "Rook" stays an author-facing name, as the Ch3 design
  asked. In the game he is **the sender** until he gives her a name, and then he is **Rafe**. (Alternatives for the
  name, if the owner prefers: Ruben Tay; Reza Karim.)
- **Who he was to Nell:** the courier she handed things to at 02:40, for three years, and then the man she trusted
  with the one thing she never logged: that she wanted out. The handoff on the leaf, the one she missed a breakfast
  for, was **the Jakarta list** (the order that burned people, and in the end her). He carried it without reading it.
  He has read it every day since.
- **The night she ran:** they were leaving together, on the Sunday ferry to Batam, from the old terminal. Meridian
  pulled him out of the country on the Saturday on a courier job he couldn't refuse. She walked the harbour wall
  toward a meeting he never came to. He does not know how she died. **That is what he wants: the truth.** He will get
  it only on this road, in Ch17, from Celeste's own mouth.
- **Why he chose Evelynn:** Meridian reissued the legend, which means Meridian would put the new Evelyn in the same
  rooms: the Vesper, the breakfast, Celeste. He could not get into those rooms. She could. He has been feeding her
  selectively for a year to steer her toward the one room where he can ask his question.
- **The pause before her name:** he knew a woman called Evelyn. He is learning, late, to say it to somebody else.
- **What it keeps consistent:** every built road. Rook is a voice on the Celebrity road and the others, and stays one;
  "Someone who knew her before you did" is simply true; the ledger's "R." is a person; the Ch6 test ("Tell me the name
  before I tell you") works because he was there for the breakfast she missed. The woman in the raincoat on the
  Celebrity road (who "isn't mine") stays that road's own mystery.

## 1. The route in one paragraph

On the Celebrity road Evelynn builds a life outside every machine. On the Institutional road she goes back inside the
one that made her. On the **Outside** road she **works for nobody, and lives on the truth somebody else chooses to
send her.** She leaves the Axiom flat for a room above a shut-down shop by the old ferry terminal; she has no badge, no
handler and no rich man's car, only a phone that rings at 02:40 and a sender who pays in documents. Every page he
sends is real, or mostly real, or real in a way that points her somewhere he wants her to go. The route asks **what a
truth is worth when you can't see who is holding it**, and what you owe the dead woman whose face you are wearing, and
the man who loved her. Its weapon is **information nobody else has**: dates, names, a ledger in a dead woman's hand.
Its pressure is **provenance**: every page might be bait, and verifying costs time she may not have. The owner's test
for every scene applies: **thrilling, erotic, fun**.

## 2. Content rules (restated, because this route leans on them)

- **Rafe never makes her Nell.** He knew a woman with her face. He never asks Evelynn to be her, dress as her or answer
  to her name, and the game never lets desire run through that confusion. If he ever says the wrong name, the scene
  stops, and it is his to repair.
- **Rafe is the route's man, and chosen intimacy with him opens only after he has told her who Nell was to him** (the
  inverse of Daniel's rule on the Institutional road: here it is **his** truth that must come first). If he never tells
  her, he stays a source and, at most, a friend, and that is complete.
- **His price is always information, never her body.** A fact for a fact, a page for a page, a question answered.
  Refusing a price costs a lead, never anything sexual.
- **The skeptic is never punished** (Ch6's rule, kept for the whole road). Every page can be verified independently.
  Cutting the source is always open, and the road stays solvable without him, only slower.
- **Sloane is a target or a trade, never a romance** (ENDGAME §4). Her charge with Evelynn is attention and power.
- **Heat 3, consent in character** (the scope choice, then stop / stay), fading at the act. The reserved coercion beat
  stays Ch13 (shared, off screen).

## 5. Endings (the Outside positions, ENDGAME §7)

- **Expose, provenance owned:** the ORACLE defect, the catalogue and the Jakarta order, published through channels
  nobody controls (Marsh's inquiry, a paper that matters, or her own name), with **her own verification** standing
  behind every page. Meridian wounded in public. She owns the risk, and signs it. The strongest outside ending, and
  the one that needs the pages verified, not just received.
- **Trade, quietly:** the defect traded for something concrete and private: Meridian's written undertaking, Rafe's
  safety, a name taken out of a catalogue. Nobody reads about it. Leverage held, the switch armed.
- **Cut the source:** she verifies everything herself, keeps only what she could prove alone, and walks away from
  Rafe and from every page she couldn't check. A free agent, owing nobody's truth.
- **Nell's name:** shared across routes. On this road it lands hardest: **Rafe is in the room, or at the door, when
  Celeste says it.**

**Rafe's endings**, by what she made him: he stays (a partner, if chosen, only after he told her; or a friend who
rings at 02:40 to say nothing is wrong); he takes Nell's file home to Singapore and to Nora; or he is cut, and sends
one last page, blank.

In every ending **Sloane ends as a person in the machine**: burned, traded, spared or, if Evelynn chose it, the one
who reads the pages out loud.

## 6. Decisions for the owner (recommendation first)

1. **Outside is the sender's road:** off the books, living on the truth a source chooses to send, with provenance as
   its pressure. *Recommended.*
2. **Who the sender is (new canon, §0):** "R." on the ledger leaf, **Rafe Lim**, Nell's courier and the man she was
   leaving with, who does not know how she died and chose Evelynn to find out. It keeps every built road consistent and
   retires the old Ch6 idea that the sender was the prior Evelyn. *Recommended.* Alternative names: Ruben Tay, Reza
   Karim.
3. **Rafe is the route's man:** a chosen partner **only after he has told her who Nell was to him**, and he **never
   makes her Nell**. If he never tells her, he stays a source. *Recommended.* Alternative: no route romance; partners
   only from the shared cast (Julian, Sebastian, Theo, Marsh).
4. **His price is always information,** the skeptic is never punished, and cutting the source is always open.
   *Recommended.*
5. **Unique chapters 7 (The Sender), 8 (Provenance) and 14 (The Source); shared framing for 10–13 and 15–18;** Ch9
   fully shared. *Recommended.*
6. **Sloane is a target or a trade** (ENDGAME §4): burn her, trade her, or spare her, decided in Ch14. *Recommended.*
7. **The endings:** expose with provenance owned, trade quietly, cut the source, or Nell's name, with Rafe hearing the
   truth about Nell in Ch17. *Recommended.*
8. **Build order:** Ch7, Ch8, Ch14 (the unique spine, with an interim bridge from Ch9 to Ch14 as the Institutional road
   had), then the shared variants in order, each chapter with its own design doc. No lane-split change is needed; the
   character entry for the sender gains a face and a name only once he gives them in play. *Recommended.*
