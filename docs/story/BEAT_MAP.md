# Beat map: Acts I–IV, chapter by chapter

The master plan for a **20–25 hour** playthrough. Every chapter has a slot, a job in the
story, an hour target and a word budget. Design and build plans cite their row here.

Built on: [CAMPAIGN_PACING_PLAN.md](CAMPAIGN_PACING_PLAN.md) (why 20–25h, the four acts),
[CONTENT_DIRECTION.md](CONTENT_DIRECTION.md) (Mature; coercion as the engine),
[CAMPAIGN_ROUTE_MAP.md](CAMPAIGN_ROUTE_MAP.md) (the diamond, the four lanes),
[ENDGAME_RECONVERGENCE.md](ENDGAME_RECONVERGENCE.md) (Celeste, Meridian, Sloane).

## How to read the budgets

- **~10,000 words per hour** of play (reading, choices, investigation screens).
- Word budgets are **words read on one path**, not total words written. A chapter with
  branches has more written than read.
- **Status:** **BUILT** (exists and is about the right size), **DEEPEN** (exists but is thin;
  grow it with new scenes, not padding), **NEW** (not built).
- **Shared** chapters are the same for every route. **Route** chapters differ by lane
  (institutional, outside, executive, own-power). Own-power is written first as the
  template (route-map decision 4).
- Current sizes are estimates: total branch words from `src/content/` (2026-09-24), with
  a single path assumed to read about half.

## Route coverage (the honest size of the build)

- **Shared:** Act I, Ch6, Ch9 (bridge) and Act IV: about 11 hours, built once.
- **Route-specific:** Ch7–8 and Act III (Ch10–15): about 10 hours **per lane**. Four lanes is
  about 40 hours of route content if each lane is fully bespoke.
- **To keep it buildable:** Act III's orders, the field op and Singapore are **shared
  scaffolding**. What differs by lane is *how she answers* (who helps, what counterplay she
  has, what it costs). Aim for about 60% shared and 40% lane-specific within route
  chapters.
- **Build order:** own-power first (already through Ch9), then one more lane end to end,
  then the other two.
