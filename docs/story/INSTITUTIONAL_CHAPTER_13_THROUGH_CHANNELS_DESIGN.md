## 1. The chapter's job

On the other roads the placement comes from Celeste on the black phone. On this road it comes **on paper, through the
proper channel, in her handler's hand**: a grey envelope, AX-7A, a tasking in Sloane's capitals with a suite number on
it. The machine that made her has learned to dress an order as procedure, and the question the whole route has been
asking arrives in its sharpest form: **when the channel is poisoned, what is a channel for?**

By the end of the chapter the player must:

1. **Receive the tasking.** A content notice opens the chapter. TASKING · THE CLAREMONT · THURSDAY 21:00 · SUBJECT: O.
   MARSH · SUITE 1109. And the last line, **BACKUP: —**, blank, where Sloane always writes "Mine." Then the black phone:
   "Victoria has sent you something, darling. Do read it carefully. And do think of Maya." The leverage is Maya's file
   (shared canon, non-sexual).
2. **Know Marsh as a person** in the week (shared canon), and one other move (Maya, Daniel if he knows, or alone).
3. **Choose the channel** (§4): take it up to seventy-one and put it on Sloane's desk; go over her head to Benton; or
   take it to nobody.
4. **Answer the order:** **comply** (the door closes, and the scene cuts); **refuse** (in writing, under the scope term if
   she has it: Axiom can't touch her; Meridian can, and Maya is detained on Friday, as canon); or **counterplay** (turn
   Marsh, a staged scene, both in on it, heat 2; or swap the camera's card, if Iris has her number).
5. **Take a recovery step,** on the comply path, under the overlay: Daniel if he knows (**being held, nothing more**),
   Maya, Sloane (only if she took it to her: Sloane sits on the floor of the hall under the green light until morning and
   does not touch her), the wall ("Done to me. Not by me."), or alone.
6. **Pin the card:** THE CLAREMONT. 1109. And the channel: SHE NEVER WROTE IT / BENTON CONFIRMED IT / NOBODY KNOWS.

The road then runs **straight into Ch14**, with no bridge.

**What it must not do:**
- show, describe or eroticize anything behind the door on the comply path;
- make any refusal cost her body. The costs are Maya's file (Meridian's) and a hearing (Axiom's, without the term);
- make Sloane complicit in the placement (she wasn't), or a romance, or a comfort that touches;
- make Daniel's or anyone's refuge sexual, a cure or a reward;
- move the placement date.

**Why it is thrilling, erotic and fun (and where it is none of those on purpose):**
- **Thrilling:** a forged order in her handler's hand, a blank backup line, and one day to decide whom to trust with it.
- **Erotic:** only in what she chooses: the staged scene with Marsh, both in on it, charged and fake.
- **Fun:** Sloane, reading a tasking with her own name on it, and going very quiet in the wrong place.
- **The comply path is not fun and not erotic, by design.** It is the cost.
