# EVE — Full Game Route Architecture

This document defines the campaign branching strategy for the full game. It does not assign final chapter numbers or replace frozen runtime revisions.

## 1. Target length

Target one substantial first-playthrough major path at **15–20 hours**.

Provisional content budget:

- **Shared foundation:** 6–7 hours
- **Route-shaped development:** 6–8 hours
- **Convergent endgame:** 3–5 hours

These are planning targets, not promises.

## 2. Route families

### Espionage / Agency
- Free Agent
- Sloane Operative
- Rook-aligned

### Corporate / Private Power
- Helix power
- Corporate Predator
- Executive Companion
- Trophy Wife
- Kept / Dependent

### Public / Body / Fame
- Celebrity Evelynn
- Public figure / editorial
- Adult Entertainment / porn-star career

### Exploitation / Recovery overlay
This can intersect any family when actual control conditions accumulate.

## 3. Diamond branching

Avoid exponentially separate campaigns.

Use:

```text
SHARED SPINE
     |
  CHOICE PRESSURE
  /     |      \
SPY   PUBLIC  EXECUTIVE
 \      |       /
   SHARED OPERATION
        |
 route-shaped consequence
        |
   later divergence
```

The diagram is a structural example, not a literal mandatory chapter map.

### Diverge
Give materially different access, scenes, relationships, adult-life opportunities, evidence, resources, body/public state, and vulnerabilities.

### Persist
Record what happened. Branch effects remain available to later scenes.

### Reconverge
Bring routes to a common crisis, investigation, location, or operation when production efficiency requires it.

### React
The shared scene changes according to persistent state.

Reconvergence must never erase the route history.

## 4. What may vary inside a shared mission

A common objective may be experienced differently through:

- entry access;
- disguise/presentation;
- public recognizability;
- companion;
- handler;
- body state;
- wardrobe;
- relationship state;
- evidence held;
- evidence custody;
- obligations;
- money/resources;
- scandal/exposure;
- attraction;
- dependency;
- who knows what;
- AdultScene eligibility.

The mission can be common while the lived experience remains route-specific.

## 5. Route crossover

Routes are not professions or factions.

Possible crossovers:

### Celebrity + Espionage
A publicly recognizable Evelynn uses press, events, staff, image management, or celebrity access to investigate while losing anonymity.

### Adult Entertainment + Corporate
Evelynn may own or manage her business, negotiate distribution, acquire capital, or become a public executive.

### Trophy Wife + Free Agent
An outwardly ornamental spouse may privately control evidence, maintain independent networks, or use elite domestic/social access operationally.

### Sloane Operative + Celebrity
Axiom may value public access while fame makes covert action harder.

### Rook-aligned + Adult Career
Industry contacts can become independent sources, cover, public leverage, or a liability.

## 6. Route progression should be cumulative

No one choice selects:
- Celebrity Route;
- Porn-Star Route;
- Trophy Wife Route;
- Kept Route;
- Corporate Predator Route.

Instead, routes emerge through repeated choices in:
- autonomy;
- appetite;
- ambition;
- visibility;
- body authorship;
- resource ownership;
- accepted obligation;
- intimacy;
- public rights;
- evidence custody;
- who supplies essential needs;
- who can impose costs.

## 7. Exploitation overlay

Exploitation is not automatically tied to:
- sex;
- adult work;
- femininity;
- fame;
- marriage;
- luxury.

It requires actual power conditions such as:
- concentrated resources;
- threats;
- blackmail;
- housing control;
- reputation control;
- contract control;
- isolation;
- bodily pressure;
- reduced exit options.

Recovery means restoring meaningful alternatives, not forcing a specific lifestyle change.

## 8. Reconvergence examples

### Shared espionage crisis
A Free Agent, celebrity, trophy wife, adult performer, and Sloane operative may all need to access the same protected event.

Their routes differ through:
- who invited them;
- who recognizes them;
- what they can wear without scrutiny;
- whether they arrive alone;
- which staff will speak to them;
- whether they can disappear afterward;
- who expects a report;
- what evidence they can safely keep.

### Shared ORACLE crisis
Different route families may converge on the same ORACLE disclosure while arriving with different:
- proof;
- allies;
- obligations;
- leverage;
- body/public history;
- trust in Sloane/Rook;
- exposure.

## 9. Replay philosophy

Replay value should come from:
- missing evidence;
- different people believing different things;
- different social access;
- alternate relationship histories;
- public/private identity differences;
- body and career choices;
- different exploitation/control states;
- route-specific scenes;
- meaningfully different endgame leverage.

Do not rely on cosmetic dialogue alone.

## 10. Production rule

When deciding whether to create a large route-exclusive sequence, ask:

1. Does it materially change the player's understanding, power, relationship, body/public state, or access?
2. Can the consequence persist into later shared scenes?
3. Can part of the environment/cast/art be reused?
4. Is reconvergence possible without invalidating the branch?
5. Does the branch justify its production cost?

If not, prefer a smaller reactive branch.
