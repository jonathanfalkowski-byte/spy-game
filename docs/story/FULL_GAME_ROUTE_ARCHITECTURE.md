# Full-Game Route Architecture

**Design only.** This document follows the [Master GDD](../design/EVE_MASTER_GDD.md). It proposes no current runtime routes, state fields, migrations, art, or save-schema changes.

## Campaign budget

| Campaign movement | Target length | Design job |
|---|---:|---|
| Shared spine | 6–7 hours | Investigative foundation, recurring evidence systems, public/private identity, and first pressure around terms and access. |
| Route development | 6–8 hours | Intelligence, public, executive, and independent trajectories acquire distinct opportunities, relationships, costs, and evidence access. |
| Convergent endgame | 3–5 hours | A shared operation tests the player’s accumulated resources and obligations while retaining route-specific consequences. |

These budgets describe a first substantial campaign, not a promise that every route receives identical scenes or an excuse to reduce routes to labels.

## Route families and crossover

| Family | Core pressure | Crossover value | What must persist |
|---|---|---|---|
| Spy / agency | Sources, secrecy, handlers, proof, operational risk | Public reach can supply access; corporate proximity can expose hidden decisions. | Evidence provenance, participant knowledge, cover risk, who controls the channel. |
| Public / celebrity / body | Image rights, authorship, audience, visibility, professional terms | Visibility can protect, compromise anonymity, or create leverage. | Publication scope, rights, name attribution, public knowledge, privacy cost. |
| Executive / private power | Access, gifts, benefits, hierarchy, care, obligation, exit cost | Executive information can serve an investigation or become a dependency risk. | Provider, term, consideration, alternatives, boundaries, what was actually accepted. |
| Free agent / Rook | Independent resources, selective trust, counter-information | May create temporary alliances with any other family. | Custody, retained evidence, competing claims, independent options. |
| Exploitation / recovery overlay | Threats, constrained alternatives, harm, restoration | Can make an existing route’s power conditions legible without defining the route. | Threat, no-alternative condition, compliance/refusal, harm, evidence, recovery step. |

## Reconvergence doctrine

A shared operation should reconverge plot logistics, not erase biography. On reconvergence, react to the route with different evidence, witnesses, resource options, public exposure, leverage, relationship vocabulary, or available exits. A route-specific consequence must be visible in play and not merely logged for an ending slide.

The investigation remains playable everywhere. Sex, intimacy, public image, adult work, luxury, and a relationship can add access or cost but cannot become the only route to required proof.

## Replay philosophy

Replays should change how the player reaches, understands, and pays for a shared operation. Useful differences include:

- who knows what, and why;
- which evidence is available and which source remains credible;
- what rights, obligations, and custody persist;
- whether Evelynn can leave an arrangement without unacceptable loss;
- public visibility and cover risk;
- relationship boundaries and prior honored or violated terms;
- recovery resources after pressure or harm.

Do not manufacture replay value by calling the same scene a different route, treating appearance as agency, or hiding a mandatory solution behind an intimate choice.
