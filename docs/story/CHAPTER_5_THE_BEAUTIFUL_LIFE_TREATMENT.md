## Rights and agency

Event photograph: only opt-in one image for Harbour programme page; no subsequent editorial license. Editorial separate named use: one Aster digital issue, thirty days, defined audience, no ads/syndication/archive reuse, no workplace endorsement. Name: Evelynn Vale or E. Vale; no Adrian binding. Image: one agreed fully clothed portrait, or text only. Player selects concept, may raise fee, review exact caption and portrait description, then authorize publication or withhold. A public record stores scope/amount/actual publication; discovered-by records require delivered links. Private proofs are not public. Erotic industry work and future exclusivity are not included.

The existing professional-only Julian can earn new mutual interest only through an explicit request and affirmative reply. His availability is narrower than a saved phone number: a completed audit and no withdrawn/false-authority access. Prior personal refusal permits a player-initiated future request, never an automatic new encounter. No pressure route restores him. Any help requires a new exact disclosure of the workroom need. He handles an inconvenient booking himself without imposing a personal price, and states the office's practical interest in a future professional contact. Service refusal does not revoke payment.

Recurring service terms are optional future commitments with written cancellation. Choosing them records ingredients for future stories, not dependency. The independent pass always survives; no account is silently closed. Money changes are deterministic and sourced; promised fees are not spendable until completed sitting or authorized issue production.
